# Shorts loop template v001 (extracted from the winner)

Source: short08 "Police Tracked His Phone for 127 Days" (video m33s6uFmXao) —
APV 276% (watched ~2.5x, loops), drove the 2026-06-28 spike (481 views/day).
Measured 2026-07-13: Shorts hooks are fine (first-3s retention ~100%); the winners
differ by holding to the END and LOOPING. Loop = the algorithm pushes reach.

## The winning structure (5 beats, ~90 words, ~41s)
1. HOOK  — "Your phone keeps a record of everywhere you go. Police wanted 127 days of it — without a warrant."
2. HOW   — "Every few minutes your phone pings a cell tower and leaves a trace of where you are."
3. STAKES— "Police pulled months of that from the carrier and placed a suspect near a string of robberies."
4. TURN  — "In 2018 the Supreme Court said no — this much data needs a warrant. For the first time, your digital trail was protected."
5. CTA   — "Watch the full story on the channel. Follow for more."

## Why it loops (the formula)
- 2nd person throughout ("your phone" / "your digital trail") = it's about ME.
- ONE shocking specific number (127 days) = a memorable anchor.
- Tight causal chain, zero filler (~90 words / 41s = brisk).
- SECRET SAUCE: the last line ECHOES the first ("your digital trail was protected"
  <-> "your phone keeps a record"). So when the video loops to the top it feels
  continuous -> viewers re-watch instead of swiping -> algorithm boosts it.

## Fill-in template (any case)
1. HOOK:  "Your <X> + <shocking specific number/fact> — <injustice: without a warrant / and it's legal>."
2. HOW:   one sentence on the mechanism (keep it "your/you"; make it credible).
3. STAKES: the real case, concrete ("Police did ___ to a real person / near real crimes").
4. TURN:  the ruling/answer — and END on a phrase that echoes the HOOK (close the loop).
5. CTA:   "Full story on the channel. Follow for more." (pinned comment does the bridging).

Specs: 35-45s, 80-100 words, ONE number as the anchor, 2nd person throughout,
loop-back ending. Brisk pace, no throat-clearing.
