## Short A — Riley v. California (2014) — searching your phone at arrest
1. HOOK:  "Get arrested for a broken taillight, and police wanted to scroll through your entire phone — no warrant."
2. HOW:   "Your photos, your texts, your location — more private data than a full search of your house, all in your pocket."
3. STAKES:"That's what happened to David Riley. Police went through his phone after a traffic stop and built a case from it."
4. TURN:  "In 2014 the Supreme Court said no — unanimously. Your phone needs a warrant. What's in your pocket stays yours."
5. CTA:   "Full story on the channel. Follow for more."
loop: pocket/phone | anchor: "a full search of your house" | title: Police Wanted to Search Your Whole Phone | cover text: YOUR PHONE?

## Short B — Mapp v. Ohio (1961) — illegal search, evidence thrown out
1. HOOK:  "Police forced into a woman's home with a warrant that didn't exist — and searched everything."
2. HOW:   "For most of American history, illegally seized evidence could still convict you. The search being illegal didn't matter."
3. STAKES:"Then, in 1957, officers waved a fake piece of paper at Dollree Mapp's door and went through her whole house."
4. TURN:  "In 1961 the Supreme Court drew the line: evidence from an illegal search can't be used — in any state. A search that breaks the rules now protects you."
5. CTA:   "Full story on the channel. Follow for more."
loop: illegal search | anchor: "a fake piece of paper" | title: They Broke In With a Fake Warrant | cover text: FAKE WARRANT

## Short C — Gideon v. Wainwright (1963) — right to a lawyer
1. HOOK:  "You're charged with a crime, can't afford a lawyer — and the court makes you defend yourself. Alone."
2. HOW:   "For most of American history, too poor to hire an attorney meant that was your problem. No money, no lawyer."
3. STAKES:"Clarence Gideon was denied one, lost, and from a prison cell handwrote his appeal to the Supreme Court — in pencil."
4. TURN:  "In 1963 they agreed, unanimously. Can't afford a lawyer? One must be given to you. That right is yours — even with nothing in your pocket."
5. CTA:   "Full story on the channel. Follow for more."
loop: can't afford a lawyer | anchor: "in pencil" | title: Too Poor for a Lawyer? This Man Fought Back | cover text: IN PENCIL
