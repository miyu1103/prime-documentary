For thirty years, the state of Alabama prepared to execute Anthony Ray Hinton for two murders he did not commit. His conviction rested on discredited ballistics testimony and a defense that could not afford a qualified expert. This is how an innocent man survived death row — and how the Equal Justice Initiative and a unanimous U.S. Supreme Court (Hinton v. Alabama, 2014) finally set him free.
