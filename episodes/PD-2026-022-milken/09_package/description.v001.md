Michael Milken built the market Wall Street dismissed as junk, became one of the most powerful financiers in America, pleaded guilty to six felony counts, paid a record penalty, served prison time, reinvented himself through philanthropy, and later received a presidential pardon.

This episode follows the full arc without flattening it into a simple hero story or a simple villain story: the high-yield bond insight, the takeover boom, the 98-count indictment, the six-count guilty plea, the lifetime securities-industry ban, the cancer diagnosis, the philanthropy, and the pardon that forgave the crime without erasing the plea.
