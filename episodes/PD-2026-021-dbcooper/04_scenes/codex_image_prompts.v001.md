## 0. ABSOLUTE HARD RULES (R2 — apply to EVERY image)
1. **NO real-person face or likeness of anyone** — the hijacker is ALWAYS faceless (from behind, shadowed, cropped, silhouette). No real crew, no real suspect, no FBI-agent likeness. No look-alikes.
2. **NO real logos / institutions / seals** — no real airline, no Boeing mark, no FBI seal, no bank. Build archetypal equivalents.
3. **NO readable text or numbers** — no signage, no serial numbers, no document text, no watermarks. Remotion adds all text.
4. **NO fake-authentic 1971 look** — nothing imitating real news footage, a real photo of the real crime, or surveillance. Symbolic cinematic reconstruction only.

## 1. STYLE BIBLE
- **Genre:** museum-grade cinematic symbolic documentary; "prestige unsolved-mystery / 1970s heist noir."
- **Palette (PD brand, strict):** deep black + midnight-navy base; **electric blue `#1F6BFF`** (cold night / forensic / investigation); **muted gold `#E5B53A`** (the cash, the legend, a warm lamp); silver highlights. Restrained filmic contrast. No candy neon, no teal-orange.
- **Light:** motivated, low-key — a cabin reading lamp, a sodium tarmac light, a flashlight beam, moonlight through rain, a desk lamp. Real falloff, deep shadow, volumetric haze. Never flat, never HDR.
- **Texture/material:** brushed aluminum, vinyl seats, rain on glass, wet asphalt, paper money, river sand, forest mist, 35mm grain. No plastic AI sheen.
- **Composition:** one clear subject; deliberate negative space for Remotion text; strong depth; parallax/Ken-Burns friendly. 16:9, long edge **>= 3840 px**.
- **Recurring motifs:** the faceless man in a dark suit and thin tie; the briefcase; banded cash; FOUR parachutes; the open aft airstair into black sky; a falling silhouette; the abandoned clip-on tie; a riverbank; a boxed case file; the empty seat.
