## What v004 changes about the film

**The ending is no longer "we could not find out". It is "neither could the court."**

Three sentences, all dated, all sourced, and they close the controlling idea exactly:

1. **30 September 2024** — a court convicts the police station chief and acquits the district
   officials, because no law required a plan for an event with no organiser (IT-30, IT-34).
2. **14 July and 28 August 2025** — two benches of the Seoul High Court **stop both appeals**,
   because a commission is still trying to establish what happened, and because fragmenting the
   procedure "aggravates the harm to the victims" (IT-84, IT-85, IT-86, IT-88).
3. **June 2026** — that commission decides it needs until **December 2027** (IT-49).

And the new opening figure for ACT_1 is the one that makes the whole film land: **81,573 people got
off the train at that station that evening, 11,873 of them in the single hour before the second
emergency call** (IT-93, IT-94). The state's own transport operator was counting them, in real time,
one stop from the alley.
