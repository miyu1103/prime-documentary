## The general lesson, recorded because it is not about this episode

A quarantine rule is only as good as a later reader's ability to find it. Three of this channel's
episodes cite quarantine rules their own ledgers never defined as rows — **EP72 cites ⛔-11 to ⛔-14
against a table that stops at ⛔-10, and EP68 cites ⛔-21 to ⛔-26 plus eighteen `AR-` and `OC-` rows
that do not exist anywhere in its ledger.** Those were found by the same tool on the same day, and
they are not this episode's to fix.

**The rule for every episode from here: a quarantine rule is a table row, or it does not exist.**
Prose next to a table is a comment.
