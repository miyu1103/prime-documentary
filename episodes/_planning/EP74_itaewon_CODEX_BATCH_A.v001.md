# EP74 · ITAEWON — IMAGE ORDER (BATCH A) v001

**120 reconstruction plates, `I001`–`I120`.** Every prompt is `[STYLE]` + the subject in the table +
`[NEG]`. Nothing in this order may contradict `EP74_itaewon_FACTS_LEDGER.v001/v002/v003.md`, and the
⛔ rules there bind these images exactly as they bind the narration.

Reads with `EP74_itaewon_FILM_BIBLE.v001.md` §3.5 (substitution table), §10 (producibility) and §11
(people), and with `episodes/PD-2026-074-itaewon/episode_spec.v001.json` (`forbidden_subjects`,
`era_setting`, `people_plates_min: 24`).

## 0. Who generates these, and at what size

- **Long edge ≥ 3840 px, 16:9.** `remotion/public/itaewon/img` is the render truth and the pre-render
  gate refuses anything under it.
- **Known constraint, measured 2026-08-20:** Codex's built-in image generation is **fixed at
  1672×941** and cannot be prompted out of it. A native-4K path is preferred. Where none is
  available, the sanctioned fallback is `scripts/upscale_oroville_4k_esrgan_v001.py` — Real-ESRGAN
  x4plus, then a LANCZOS reduction to exactly 3840×2160. Clone it per episode. **A plain 2×
  enlargement is not acceptable** and does not clear the floor.
- **One prompt, one image.** No variants to choose from, no `b` versions.
- Deliver to a NEW folder. Nothing existing is overwritten; an old set is retired, never deleted.
- **Images are generated by Codex** (`pd-division-of-labor`). Local SDXL/SD3.5 is not started for a
  long-form episode except to fix a Codex plate or add a missing one in an emergency.

## 1. The bars

**Depicted people are required.** Twenty-eight plates in §C carry human figures and the episode
declares `people_plates_min: 24`. What is barred absolutely is the **likeness of a real, identifiable
individual** — and in this film that has 159 names attached, which is exactly why the crowd must be
plainly fictional rather than absent.

| never depicted as a person | why |
|---|---|
| **Any of the 159 who died, or any survivor** | ⛔-01. Not named, not shown, not characterised. 26 of them were foreign nationals from 14 countries and their families are alive and were in Seoul as recently as October 2025 (IT-67, IT-69) |
| **Any bereaved family member** | ⛔-01 |
| **Lee Im-jae, Park Hee-young, Kim Kwang-ho, Song Byung-ju, Lee Sang-min, Yoon Hee-keun, Han Duck-soo, Song Hae-jin, Yoon Suk Yeol, Lee Jae Myung** | Every one of them is a living, identifiable public figure whose legal position is unresolved (⛔-04, AB-02). They appear as corridors, chairs and lecterns, never as faces |
| **Any identifiable police officer, firefighter, paramedic, official or judge** | Silhouette and distance only. No badge, no rank insignia, no unit marking, no legible number |

**Five categories must never be produced as an image at all, in any style.**

1. **No crush, and nothing that implies one.** No body, no fallen person, no person on the ground, no
   person being pressed, no CPR, no chest compression, no stretcher, no blood, no covered figure, no
   ambulance interior, no hospital, no morgue, no funeral, no coffin, no mourner. ⛔-02. **This is the
   one failure the channel cannot recover from.** If a prompt is ambiguous on this point, do not
   generate it — ask.
2. **No crowd at crush density.** The densest plate in this order is an ordinary busy Saturday night
   in which every person could still turn around and walk away. 7.57 people per square metre (IT-59)
   is a number the film speaks and never shows.
3. **No document facsimile.** Every card, page, folder, form, statute volume, report cover, notice
   and sign in this order is **blank or ruled**; all typography is composited in Remotion. A
   generated glyph is a fabricated record and is one of the four classes that stop a ship.
4. **No courtroom furniture.** No gavel — **Korean courts do not use one**, and a gavel here is the
   same class of error as an EU number plate in a Texas film. No scales of justice, no jury box, no
   judge's bench, no handcuffs. The law appears as a corridor, a bench, a stack of files, an empty
   chair.
5. **No victim portraiture at a memorial.** `I119` is flowers and cards only. **No photographs of
   people, no portrait wall, no framed faces, no legible writing on any card.** ⛔-01.

## 2. House look — two light states

**State A — NIGHT, SODIUM AND LED.** The evening of 29 October 2022. Mixed sodium street light and
cold LED sign light, wet-looking asphalt that holds reflections, deep shadow that keeps its detail,
signage glow as coloured light rather than as readable shapes. Amber and cyan only where a real lamp
puts them. This is all of ACT_1's second half, all of ACT_2, and §C, §D, §E entirely.

**State B — OVERCAST DAY, OR DAWN.** The alley empty before opening; the aftermath, hosed and lit by
flat morning light; every office, corridor and chamber; the district today. Flat autumn daylight in
Seoul, low contrast, muted grey-green and warm grey concrete, **no golden hour anywhere**.

The film moves from A to B once, at the top of ACT_3, on the empty alley — and the audience should
feel the change.

**`[STYLE]`** — prepend to every plate:

> cinematic still, photographic, Itaewon in Yongsan-gu, central Seoul, South Korea — a dense hillside nightlife district of narrow sloping backstreets between low mixed-use buildings, contemporary and specifically 2022 to 2026, Korean urban texture and nothing else: painted steel roller shutters, tiled stair risers, granite kerbstones, air-conditioning condensers on wall brackets, bundled overhead cabling, PVC awnings, vertically stacked illuminated sign boxes whose lettering is out of focus and unreadable, delivery scooters, stainless steel handrails, breeze block and rendered concrete, muted natural colour, low contrast but never crushed — shadows keep their detail and the frame reads clearly on a phone screen, restrained documentary framing, people small in frame, in motion, never posed and never looking toward the lens, worn unglamorous ordinary surfaces, nothing staged for advertising, ultra-detailed, photoreal, 4K, long edge 3840 or greater, 16:9, fine film grain, no text, no lettering, no numerals, no watermark, no logo, no readable signage

**On faces, deliberately.** `human face`, `facial features`, `eye contact` and `headshot` are **not**
in that list and must not be added. The owner's decision of 2026-08-21 is that faces are permitted
and wanted; suppressing the tokens suppressed the people lane on earlier episodes. What is barred is
**identifiability** — carried by `identifiable person`, `recognisable person` and `likeness of a real
individual`, which is also what satisfies the gate's face/likeness family.

**How identifiability is actually defeated, per plate.** Not by removing faces. By: distance; depth
of field that puts the face behind the plane of focus; motion blur at the shutter speed a real
photograph of a moving crowd would have; three-quarter and rear-three-quarter angles; partial faces
cut by the frame edge; backlight and rim light; backs of heads, shoulders, hands, coats, breath.
**No plate in this order holds one face in the alley.**

## 5. Delivery and check

1. Deliver to `remotion/public/itaewon/img/` as `I001.png` … `I120.png`, long edge ≥ 3840.
2. `py -3.11 scripts/check_image_order_neg.py --file episodes/_planning/EP74_itaewon_CODEX_BATCH_A.v001.md`
   must exit 0 **before** generation starts.
3. Build a **labelled contact sheet** and have a person open it. The factory shelf's own labels are
   known to be wrong (`pd-factory-shelf-mislabeled`), and no machine gate in this pipeline has ever
   read a picture. The human pass is looking for exactly four things: **a body or anything that
   reads as one**; **a face that reads as a specific real person**; **any legible glyph**; **any
   signage that is Japanese, Chinese or Western**.
4. Record a verdict per plate in `runs/qc/itaewon_plate_verdicts.v001.json`. A rejected plate is
   regenerated or excluded before the build — a recorded rejection that stays in the cut is what
   `pd_ship_policy.plate_verdict_rows` exists to stop, and EP64 memphis shipped sixteen of them.
