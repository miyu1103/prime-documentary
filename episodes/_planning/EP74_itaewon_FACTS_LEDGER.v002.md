## What v002 changes about how the film is built

1. **ACT_2 gains a clock.** The second call is **20:09**, not "about ninety minutes later" (IT-63,
   IT-65). Every call beat can now sit on a real time.
2. **ACT_3 gains its physics, and it is measured rather than described.** 7.57 people per square
   metre, 9.95 at maximum, 1,063 newtons per metre of pressure, 1,961 at maximum, and a
   **bidirectional collision** as the mechanism (IT-59, IT-60, IT-62). None of it requires showing
   anything, which is exactly what ⛔-02 needs.
3. **ACT_3 gains the thirteen minutes** — 22:15 to 22:28 (IT-63, IT-64) — which is the sourced,
   non-graphic way to carry the response without a single frame of it.
4. **A new act-length region exists between ACT_3 and ACT_4: the minister.** February 2023, an
   impeachment; July 2023, nine judges, unanimous, reinstated (IT-71–IT-73). The court's own reason
   is that the response system "was not significantly deficient" — which sits directly against the
   audit of October 2025 (IT-10) and against the commission's 2026 finding (IT-25). **Three state
   bodies, three answers, in that order.** That is the strongest new material in this file and it
   belongs in ACT_5.
5. **The ending gains a scale.** 909 cameras across 71 areas (IT-77), next to a plastic barrier at
   knee height (IT-50). The state's answer is both enormous and knee-high, and the film can now say
   so with two sourced numbers.
6. **The dead gain a shape without a single name**: 26 people from 14 countries among the 159; 46
   relatives of 21 of them flown in by the government three years later (IT-67, IT-69). ⛔-01 is
   untouched — nobody is named, shown or characterised.
