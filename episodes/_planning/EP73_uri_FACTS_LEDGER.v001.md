## 1. THE ISLAND — why Texas is on its own

| id | fact | grade | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| TX-01 | In **1935** the Federal Power Act tasked a federal commission with overseeing **electricity sales that crossed state lines** | VERBATIM | SRC-0006 |
| TX-02 | Texas utilities responded by keeping power **inside the state's borders**: if no power crossed a state line, the sale was not interstate commerce and the federal government could not regulate their rates or operations | VERBATIM | SRC-0006 |
| TX-03 | The policy of isolation was **written into an agreement** among the utilities, binding themselves to intrastate operation | SECONDARY | SRC-0006 |
| TX-04 | **ERCOT was formed in 1970**, five years after the 1965 blackout that shut down much of the north-eastern United States, and the Texas grid remains outside the reach of the federal regulator | SECONDARY | SRC-0006 |

## 3. THE FOUR MINUTES AND TWENTY-THREE SECONDS

| id | fact | grade | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| TX-10 | ERCOT ordered load shedding at **01:20 Central Time on 15 February 2021** to prevent the risk of a system-wide blackout | VERBATIM | SRC-0007 |
| TX-11 | Plants continued to fail through that hour, prompting further load shedding and pushing grid frequency **below 59.4 Hz for four minutes and twenty-three seconds** | VERBATIM | SRC-0007 |
| TX-12 | Had frequency stayed below 59.4 Hz for **nine minutes or more**, more generation would have tripped, risking a system-wide shutdown that could have cut power to millions more, **significantly slowed restoration and damaged units across the grid** | VERBATIM | SRC-0007 |
| TX-13 | ERCOT ordered a total of **20,000 MW** of rolling blackouts — **the largest manually controlled load shedding event in United States history** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001, SRC-0007 |
| TX-14 | **More than 4.5 million people** in Texas lost power, some for as long as **four days** | VERBATIM | SRC-0007 |

## 4. THE LOOP — the mechanism at the centre of the film

| id | fact | grade | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| TX-15 | Natural gas production in Texas **dropped by about 45 %** | SECONDARY | SRC-0007 |
| TX-16 | Of the natural gas fuel-supply problems, **43.3 % were caused by freezing temperatures and weather** and **21.5 % by power losses at midstream, wellhead or gathering facilities** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 |
| TX-17 | **Most natural gas production and processing facilities in ERCOT were not identified as critical load**, so the controlled load shedding **took their power away** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 |
| TX-18 | Cutting power to those facilities **took fuel sources offline, which forced further load shedding** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 |
| TX-19 | Some facilities shut in to avoid freezing; others were taken offline by frozen equipment or by having their power cut | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 |
| TX-20 | The final report contains **28 formal recommendations**, including revisions to the NERC reliability standards on **generator winterization and gas–electric coordination** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 |

**This is the film's engine, in one sentence, and it must be narrated as the report states it:** the
grid shed load to save itself, the load it shed included the gas facilities that fuelled it, and the
loss of that fuel forced it to shed more.

## ⛔ QUARANTINE — assertions that must never appear

| id | rule |
|---|---|
| ⛔-01 | Never say **winterization was legally required** of Texas generators before 2021. It was **recommended** in 2011, **NERC declined to make it a standard** (TX-06, TX-07), and the requirement arrived afterwards in SB 3 (TX-37) |
| ⛔-02 | Never say **the grid collapsed**. It did not. Load was shed to prevent a collapse and the margin is a measured number: **four minutes and twenty-three seconds against a nine-minute threshold** (TX-11, TX-12) |
| ⛔-03 | Never blame **a single fuel type**. The record attributes freeze-related outages across fuel types and attributes a large share of the shortfall to **gas supply**, including gas facilities that lost power because the grid shed it (TX-16, TX-17) |
| ⛔-04 | Never state a death toll other than **246** as fact. Alternative estimates are narrated **with the outlet named and the word "estimate"** (TX-26, TX-30) |
| ⛔-05 | **No victim is named, shown or characterised.** No household, no bill, no bereavement belonging to an identifiable person unless it is sourced and attributed |
| ⛔-06 | Never give **a single figure for the economic cost**. Two ranges exist and are not reconciled (TX-36). Either give both with attribution, or give none |
| ⛔-07 | The **"loophole"** characterisation (TX-42) is what named senators said, not a finding. Narrate it as their criticism or not at all |
| ⛔-08 | Never assert that an interconnection to the national grids would have prevented this. It is a live policy argument, not a record |

## The one-sentence contradiction this film is built on

> Ten years earlier the same thing happened on a smaller scale, a federal report recommended that
> power plants be built to survive the cold, the standard was never made — and when it happened
> again the grid saved itself by cutting power to the gas fields that fuelled it.

Every act serves that sentence.
