## 2. WHAT NIST FOUND (published 22 June 2026)

| ID | Claim | Grade | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| CT-04 | The investigation is the **National Construction Safety Team (NCST)** investigation; investigative co-leads **Judith Mitrani-Reiser** and **Glenn Bell**. | ✓ | NIST investigation page |
| CT-05 | **Two connections between garage columns and the pool deck failed in early June 2021**, initiating a cascading failure **that spread over about three weeks**. | ✓ | NIST press release |
| CT-06 | The mechanism was a **punching shear failure** at two columns in the garage under the pool-deck slab; their load transferred to elements not strong enough to carry it. | ✓ | NIST press release |
| CT-07 | ✓ **VERBATIM — Judith Mitrani-Reiser:** *"When building structures are designed and built to required codes and standards, they have margins against failure, meaning they should be able to support much more load than they are expected to bear. In the case of Champlain Towers South, however, these margins against failure were too narrow from the start."* | ✓ VERBATIM | NIST press release |
| CT-08 | ✓ **VERBATIM — Glenn Bell:** *"The low margins against failure were primarily caused by two factors… First, severe and widespread deviations in the building's original structural design from the codes and standards of the day, but also some limitations in those codes and standards. ## 6. DIGNITY AND AD-SAFETY (gate 6 — proposal for owner ruling)

**Proposal:** name **no individual victim** on screen or in narration. Reasons: (a) 98 people, so any selection is an editorial judgment about whose death is more usable; (b) the film's subject is the record, not the deaths; (c) it removes an entire class of family-consent and accuracy risk at no narrative cost — the number does the work. **The named people in this film are the ones who wrote things down**: Morabito, Chouela, Prieto, Wodnicki, Mitrani-Reiser, Bell, Hanzman. All are named for documented acts, none for their character.

**Hard bans:** no recovery or rescue footage of bodies; no recreation of any identified person's final moments; no fabricated bodycam, security-camera or timestamp authenticity (R-39, invariant 11); AI visuals are symbolic reconstruction, labelled and disclosed. Living named people are described only by what the record shows them doing or saying — **no attribution of motive or state of mind to anyone**, which also keeps the film inside the R2 causation locks.
