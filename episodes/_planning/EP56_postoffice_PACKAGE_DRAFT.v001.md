## 7. Living-persons / legal note (what the title, thumbnail, description, tags and pinned comment may NEVER claim)

- **The sub-postmasters are INNOCENT — state it as fact.** Their convictions were quashed by the Court of Appeal (Hamilton & Others [2021] EWCA Crim 577, 23 April 2021 — prosecutions "so egregious as to make the prosecution of any of the 'Horizon cases' an affront to the conscience of the court") and by the Post Office (Horizon System) Offences Act 2024, s.1(1): "Every conviction to which this Act applies is quashed on the coming into force of this Act." **Never hedge** ("accused of", "allegedly innocent", "claimed the system was at fault" are all wrong). Equally: **no packaging element may suggest any sub-postmaster actually stole anything** (gate R-INNOCENT).
- **NOBODY has been criminally convicted for the scandal** (date-stamped: NPCC Operation Olympos update, 26 May 2026). Packaging may state this plainly and in the present tense. It may **not** predict, imply or promise future charges, arrests or prosecutions, and it may not present the police investigation as an imminent reckoning.
- **Paula Vennells is LIVING and has never been charged with any offence.** No title, thumbnail, tag, description line or pinned comment may name her, depict her, or imply criminality, dishonesty as a criminal act, or personal guilt. What is safely statable, and only in the film's narration with attribution, is the record: the 30 January 2015 email quoted at *Bates No 6* §§520–526, her three days of inquiry evidence (22–24 May 2024), "I was too trusting", the 1.2-million-signature petition, her return of the CBE on 9 January 2024, and The Gazette notice 4558744 recording that the appointment was "cancelled and annulled". **The CBE forfeiture is an honours-system decision, not a criminal finding** — packaging must never blur the two. She is deliberately absent from the tag list.
- **Gareth Jenkins is LIVING and uncharged.** The Court of Appeal's findings about his evidence are adjudicated and may be narrated with attribution; he is never named in packaging and never labelled a liar, fraudster or criminal.
- **The Act quashed convictions — it did not convict anyone.** Never phrase the 2024 Act as punishment, as a verdict against the Post Office, or as "justice served". Its own constitutional controversy (Lord Burnett: "Parliament does not quash convictions; that is a matter for the courts") is the honest frame.
- **Institutional attribution only.** "THE POST OFFICE KNEW" / "THEY KNEW IN 2010" are permissible because the Court of Appeal found exactly that of the institution (Hamilton §121: "POL knew that there were problems with Horizon"). The same sentence attached to any named individual is **not** permitted.
- **Fujitsu is a live trading entity and still the Horizon contractor** (contract extended to 2027). Packaging may state the system was built and maintained by Fujitsu and that the Inquiry found "wholly unacceptable behaviour… by the Post Office and Fujitsu as institutions" (Vol 1 §1.9). It may **not** allege corporate crime, fraud, or a cover-up as a legal characterisation, and it may not use Fujitsu's mark or livery.
- **Numbers carry the ledger's hedges everywhere except the title's compression.** "Roughly a thousand prosecuted and convicted" is the Inquiry's own finding (Vol 1 §3.24); **236 imprisoned and ~700 Post-Office-brought prosecutions are BBC research** and are attributed in the description ("by the BBC's count") and in narration. Never use "983". Never state a single hard "quashed by the Act" total beyond the MoJ management-information framing (619 individuals as of 30 June 2026, date-stamped, or "more than six hundred").

It appears **nowhere** in the title, thumbnail, tags, pinned comment or the description's first two lines.
