## 0. Why this file carries citations, and why that is not decoration

`config/ship_policy.v001.json` puts **title and thumbnail text inside the blocking `factual_support`
class.** A false title is not a taste problem that gets logged as a deviation — it closes the door. And
the reason is measured: on 2026-08-11 a repackaging pass produced two titles that were **false and passed
every mechanical check** — length, form, punctuation, case — because no automatic rule can detect a lie
that is the right shape. The ship policy's own honest self-assessment records that there is **no machine
check at all** that compares a title's assertion to the script.

So the rule for this episode: **every figure, name and outcome below is followed by the ledger row or the
script line it comes from.** A claim that cannot be cited does not go on a title or a thumbnail, and a
claim graded uncertain never reaches a thumbnail.

## 1. The actor trap, which this case has in a sharp form

**This is a FEDERAL case about an FBI raid.** The looseness that would dissolve the film's own argument:

| Wrong | Right | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| "police raided" | **the FBI raided** | a six-member **FBI** SWAT team led by an **FBI** special agent (F-03) |
| "Atlanta police raided" | Atlanta Police Department officers were **in the caravan and staged outside**; the SWAT team was the FBI's | the Atlanta officers are the source of the only real footage that exists, and they did not breach the door (J02) |
| "the state" / "Georgia" / "the city" | **the United States** | the defendant is the United States, sued under the Federal Tort Claims Act (L-03) |
| "the government took" | the United States **asserted immunity** | nothing was taken; a remedy was refused (H-01, ⛔-13) |
| "the court ruled against them" (of the Supreme Court) | the Supreme Court **ruled for them, unanimously, on two questions, and decided nothing else** | L-17, L-19, L-20 |

Georgia state law does appear in this story — it supplies the tort standard the FTCA borrows — and that
is exactly why the loose phrasing is dangerous: *Georgia law* is in the case, *Georgia* is not the
defendant.

| 要素 | 開始F | 終了F | 移動量 | イージング | opacity |

| モノグラム | 0 | 12 | translateY **+40 → 0 px** ／ scale **0.94 → 1.0** | `spring({fps, config:{damping: 18, mass: 0.5}})` | 0 → 1（併用） |
