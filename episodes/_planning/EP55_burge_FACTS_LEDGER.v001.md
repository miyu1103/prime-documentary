## ★ CONFIRMED FACTS

### The commander & the machine (Act I)
- **BU-01 (H)** **Jon Graham Burge**, born **December 20, 1947**, Chicago; raised in **South Deering** on the city's Southeast Side (NOT Archer Heights — corrected this session). Died **September 19, 2018**, Florida (Tampa Bay area), **age 70**.
- **BU-02 (H)** Army service 1966–69 (+ Reserve): South Korea, then Vietnam; from June 18, 1968 with the **Ninth Military Police Company, Ninth Infantry Division**. Decorations: **Bronze Star, Purple Heart**, Vietnamese Cross of Gallantry, two Army Commendation Medals. Honorably discharged August 25, 1969.
- **BU-03 (H · attribute the inference)** **Vietnam link to the "black box":** fellow veterans of his company told reporters (Conroy/Chicago Reader; later Tribune) they witnessed or heard of prisoner interrogations using **hand-cranked field telephones to deliver electric shock** — and Area 2 victims later described a strikingly similar hand-cranked device. **Burge always denied torturing anyone or witnessing torture in Vietnam; nothing documents that he personally used or learned the technique there.** USAGE: "men who served in his unit said…" — the causal link is INFERENCE, always attributed, never asserted.
- **BU-04 (H)** CPD career: joined **March 1970** (age 22); **detective May 1972** (Area 2, South Side); **sergeant 1977**; **lieutenant 1981**, the year he took command of the newly formed **Area 2 Violent Crimes unit**; commander of Bomb & Arson 1986; commanded the **Area 3 detective division from 1988**.
- **BU-05 (H)** **"Midnight Crew"** = the documented nickname for the (white) detectives working under Burge at Areas 2/3. Torture era as framed by federal prosecutors: **1972–1991**.
- **BU-06 (H · ⚠ NEVER depicted, only described)** Methods documented across the Goldston OPS report, the Wilson litigation, the 2006 special prosecutors' report, and the TIRC record: **electric shock** to genitals, ears, nose, fingers via a hand-cranked field-telephone-type **"black box"** (physically introduced as evidence in the Wilson civil trials) and a plug-in shock device; **suffocation with plastic typewriter covers and bags** ("bagging"); **mock executions / guns in mouths**; **Russian roulette** (Darrell Cannon case, attributed to a named detective — we use "his men"); **beatings with phone books, rubber hoses, flashlights**; **radiator burns** (Wilson — medically documented second-degree burns). NUANCE: black box + radiator burns are the most rigorously court-documented (Wilson); cattle prod & Russian roulette rest on sustained victim testimony credited by OPS/special prosecutors. USAGE: describe clinically; the FILM never shows any of it (objects/aftermath only).
- **BU-07 (H)** Victim count — state as a range with attribution: **"more than 100 Black men — at least 118 by his victims' lawyers' count."** Anchors: Goldston report named **50** alleged victims (1990); People's Law Office documented list = **118**; Invisible Institute's Chicago Police Torture Archive documents **~120** (1972–1991); PLO's Joey Mogul: "about 115 known victims." Victims were **almost all Black men**. ⚠ CAUTION: TIRC's 600+ claims include post-2016 NON-Burge Cook County cases — never cite TIRC totals as a Burge victim count.

### The screams nobody heard (Act II)
- **BU-08 (H · ⚠ two murdered officers — dignity)** **February 9, 1982:** Chicago police officers **William Fahey and Richard O'Brien** shot and killed during a traffic stop. The manhunt that followed was massive and violent.
- **BU-09 (H · ⚠)** **February 14, 1982:** **Andrew Wilson** arrested and taken to Area 2. Over ~13+ hours he was beaten, **electric-shocked with the black box** (ears, nose, fingers, genitals), **burned against a radiator** (documented second-degree burns, chest/thigh), had a gun put in his mouth. He arrived at the lockup/jail with roughly **15 documented injuries**. (Wilson was convicted of the officers' murders — he was NOT innocent of the crime; the case is about what was done to him and what it exposed. State this honestly.)
- **BU-10 (H · THE COLD OPEN)** **February 17, 1982:** **Dr. John Raba**, medical director of Cermak Health Services at Cook County Jail, examined Wilson and wrote to Police Supt. **Richard Brzeczek** detailing the injuries and demanding **"a thorough investigation of this alleged brutality."** **February 25, 1982:** Brzeczek forwarded Raba's letter to Cook County State's Attorney **Richard M. Daley**, writing that he would not investigate absent guidance, given the pending prosecution. **Daley's office never responded; no investigation of any kind followed** (a failure the 2006 special prosecutors' report explicitly faulted Daley for). The letter chain is solidly documented.
- **BU-11 (H)** Wilson convicted and sentenced to **death 1983**; **Illinois Supreme Court reversed April 1987** (confession coerced — the burns matched the radiator); retried, convicted June 1988, life without parole. Died in prison **2007**.
- **BU-12 (H)** Wilson's federal civil suits (People's Law Office — G. Flint Taylor, Jeff Haas, John Stainthorp): two 1989 trials — the first hung; the second produced a perverse special verdict (the city had a de facto policy of abusing cop-killing suspects, yet the officers were exonerated). **Seventh Circuit (Judge Posner) reversed in 1993** (Wilson v. City of Chicago, 6 F.3d 1233). On remand, judgment against Burge; roughly **$1 million** total in judgments/fees paid by the City — but **Wilson himself effectively received none of it** (fees/offsets). (M on the exact dollar mechanics — hedge as "about a million dollars, which he never saw.")
- **BU-13 (H)** **"Deep Badge":** during the 1989 civil trial, an anonymous source inside Area 2 sent Wilson's lawyers **four letters** (postmarked Feb 2, Mar 6, Mar 15, Jun 16, 1989) pointing to **Melvin Jones** and other victims and naming names; the leads led PLO to ~25 more Area 2 victims.
- **BU-14 (H)** **Melvin Jones:** arrested **February 5, 1982** — nine days before Wilson — shocked by Burge with the black box on his foot, thigh and genitals; his suppression-hearing record became the crucial "prior torture" corroboration. After the 2010 verdict: *"I got justice, something I have been wanting for 28 years."*
- **BU-15 (H · ⚠ living)** **Anthony Holmes**, arrested **May 1973** — one of the earliest known victims; black-box shocks and bagging until he blacked out repeatedly; confessed; served ~30 years; paroled 2004. His 2010 trial testimony (verbatim, of record): **"He plugged it into the wall and came towards me… He said, 'You're going to tell us what we want to know.'"** / **"I didn't know if it was daytime or nighttime. He just kept shocking me."** At sentencing: **"I don't hate him. I hate what he did to me, and I hate the fact that nobody would listen to me when I tried to tell people about it; it fell on deaf ears."**
- **BU-16 (H)** **John Conroy**, Chicago Reader: **"House of Screams," January 25, 1990** — the first major exposé (on the Wilson civil trials). It was the first of **23 articles** Conroy wrote on Area 2 torture over ~17 years while the wider press and the city largely looked away. (Correct "a dozen" → 23.)
- **BU-17 (H · QUOTE SOURCE)** **The Goldston report** (OPS investigator Michael Goldston, dated **September 28, 1990**): found abuse at Area 2 spanning 1973–1986 "did occur," was **"systematic"**, "methodical," and **"included psychological techniques and planned torture"**; command members, "particularly Jon Burge," were aware of it. Named **50** alleged victims. A companion OPS report (Francine Sanders) sustained the Wilson allegations; OPS recommended firing Burge, Yucaitis and O'Hara (Sept 26, 1990). **The City suppressed the report until a federal court ordered its release in February 1992.**
- **BU-18 (H)** Burge suspended **November 8, 1991**; **FIRED by the Chicago Police Board in February 1993** for the physical abuse of Andrew Wilson (sources split on Feb 10 vs Feb 11 — say **"February 1993"**). Detectives **John Yucaitis and Patrick O'Hara were NOT fired** — suspended 15 months each, returned to duty. Burge was never criminally charged at this point. He moved to **Apollo Beach, Florida** (Tampa Bay — **NOT Apopka**, corrected this session), bought a house in 1994, and kept boats — famously a 40-foot cabin cruiser named **"Vigilante"** (court-attested; at trial he testified he picked the name "at random"). He did not "live on" the boat — he owned it.

### The clock runs out (Act III)
- **BU-19 (H)** **Statute of limitations:** Illinois' 3-year limitations period on the relevant state crimes (aggravated battery, official misconduct, perjury) had expired long before the evidence was assembled — the reason no torture prosecution was ever possible. U.S. Attorney **Patrick Fitzgerald**, Oct 2008 (verbatim): **"The statute of limitations on any crimes that happened in the 1980s or 1993, they're gone."**
- **BU-20 (H)** **Special prosecutors Edward Egan & Robert Boyle:** appointed **April 2002** by Presiding Judge Paul Biebel; 292-page report released **July 19, 2006** after 4 years and about **$7 million**. Reviewed **148** abuse claims; concluded torture occurred (they believed it in about half the cases examined) and found **proof beyond a reasonable doubt in exactly 3 cases — Andrew Wilson, Phillip Adkins, Alfonzo Pinex** — every one barred by limitations. **Charges: zero.** (Report widely criticized as too cautious — one line max.)
- **BU-21 (H/M)** **The Death Row 10:** condemned men whose convictions rested on Area 2 tortured confessions; organized from death row ~1997–98 with the Campaign to End the Death Penalty (Ronald Kitchen a co-founder; his mother Louva Bell organized outside; leaflets typed in cells; Frank Bounds died of cancer on death row in 1998). Roster (M — law-review/activist sourcing; count sometimes 10–13): Hobley, Patterson, Howard, Kitchen, Orange, Kidd, Maxwell, Bounds, R. Mahaffey, J. Mahaffey. USAGE: "the Death Row 10" as a movement; name only the four pardonees + Kitchen individually.
- **BU-22 (H)** **Gov. George Ryan:** execution moratorium **January 2000**. **Friday, January 10, 2003** (DePaul College of Law speech): **pardoned on grounds of innocence four Burge torture survivors — Aaron Patterson, Madison Hobley, Leroy Orange, Stanley Howard.** ⚠ Their innocence is legally established — statable as fact. (Nuance: Howard remained held on a separate conviction.) **Saturday, January 11, 2003** (Northwestern Law): commuted the death sentences of **167 condemned inmates** (164 to life; 3 to 40 years; some outlets say 163 then on death row — word carefully). Ryan verbatim (DPIC transcript): **"Our capital system is haunted by the demon of error: error in determining guilt, and error in determining who among the guilty deserves to die."**
- **BU-23 (H)** **Ronald Kitchen:** sentence commuted 2003; charges dropped and released **July 7, 2009**; later certificate of innocence — the one Death Row 10 name beyond the four whose full exoneration is safe to state.
- **BU-24 (H)** While all this ground on, Burge lived in Apollo Beach on a **CPD pension of roughly $3,000 a month**, fishing from the "Vigilante." (Pension amount H — multi-source.)

### Lying under oath → the payoff cascade (Act IV — stack LAST)
- **BU-25 (H)** **The lie:** in **Madison Hobley's federal civil-rights suit (Hobley v. Burge)**, Burge signed **written answers to interrogatories in November 2003** denying he had ever used or been aware of torture or abuse at Area 2. That signature — not the torture — became the crime.
- **BU-26 (H)** Federal grand jury indictment returned **October 16, 2008**, unsealed **October 21, 2008**, the day FBI agents arrested Burge at his **Apollo Beach, Florida** home. Charges: **TWO counts of obstruction of justice + ONE count of perjury** (★corrected this session — NOT 2 perjury + 1 obstruction). Fitzgerald (DOJ release, verbatim): **"There is no place for torture and abuse in a police station. There is no place for perjury and false statements in federal lawsuits."**
- **BU-27 (H)** Trial May–June 2010, N.D. Ill., **Judge Joan Humphrey Lefkow**; torture survivors including Anthony Holmes and Melvin Jones testified. **Convicted June 28, 2010, on all three counts.** Fitzgerald after the verdict: **"It's a measure of justice; it's not a perfect sense of justice."**
- **BU-28 (H · QUOTE SOURCE)** **Sentenced January 21, 2011: 4½ years (54 months)** — the probation office had recommended 15–21 months. Lefkow verbatim (of record): **"How can one trust the system of justice when the system is so defiled?"** and that his conduct **"demonstrates at the very least a severe lack of respect for the due process of law."** Burge at sentencing: **"I am 63 years old, and while I try to keep a proud face, in reality I am a broken man."** Reported to **FCI Butner (NC)** by March 16, 2011; halfway house (Tampa area) early **October 2014**; **fully released February 13, 2015** (~3.5 years in custody with good-conduct credit).
- **BU-29 (H)** **Pension kept — the 4–4 deadlock:** **January 2011**, days after sentencing, the Policemen's Annuity and Benefit Fund board split **4–4** — the four police-officer trustees voted to let Burge keep the pension, the four civilian appointees voted to strip it; a tie = no majority to terminate. AG Lisa Madigan sued; the **Illinois Supreme Court (July 3, 2014, People ex rel. Madigan v. Burge)** held the AG could not challenge the board's decision — Burge kept his pension for life, collecting it until the day he died. (4–4 board vote: H. The court's own 4–3 split: M — don't use.)
- **BU-30 (H · QUOTE SOURCE · the unrepentance)** **April 2015** — days after the city agreed to the reparations fund — Burge broke his silence in a statement to a police blog (The Conviction Project, run by then-officer Martin Preib), verbatim: **"I find it hard to believe that the city's political leadership could even contemplate giving 'Reparations' to human vermin."** (⚠ The phrase "so-called victims" is NOT verified verbatim — never quote it.) He never apologized for or admitted the torture; at sentencing he apologized only for bringing the department "into disrepute."
- **BU-31 (H · THE SUMMIT)** **May 6, 2015:** Chicago City Council **unanimously** passed the Burge reparations ordinance — per Amnesty International, **the first time a U.S. municipality offered reparations for law-enforcement violence** (safe phrasing: "the first municipal reparations package for police violence in U.S. history"). The package: **$5.5 million fund → 57 vetted living survivors** (up to $100,000 each; ≈$96,500 actual); a **formal apology**; **free tuition at the City Colleges of Chicago for survivors, their children AND grandchildren**; specialized trauma counseling → the **Chicago Torture Justice Center, opened 2017 in Englewood**; the history **taught in Chicago Public Schools** (BU-33); and a commitment to a **public memorial** (BU-34). Campaign: Chicago Torture Justice Memorials (with Project NIA, Amnesty, We Charge Genocide); attorney **Joey Mogul drafted the ordinance**; introduced by Ald. Moreno & Brookins Oct 2013; it sat in committee until 2015.
- **BU-32 (H)** Mayor **Rahm Emanuel**, May 6, 2015 (verbatim): **"This is another step but an essential step in righting a wrong, removing a stain on the reputation of this great city."** (Also of record: "A payment's one thing, but being honest and saying you're sorry and accountable is also another thing." Survivors/activists called the apology half-hearted — one honest line allowed.)
- **BU-33 (H)** **The curriculum:** **"Reparations Won: A Case Study in Police Torture, Racism, and the Movement for Justice in Chicago"** — mandated for **8th and 10th grade** in Chicago Public Schools; announced **September 2017** (survivors Darrell Cannon and Anthony Holmes present), **first taught spring 2018**. USAGE: "announced 2017, in classrooms from 2018" — Chicago now teaches its children what Burge did.
- **BU-34 (H · UPDATED THIS SESSION — no longer merely "delayed")** **The memorial:** design **"Breath, Form & Freedom"** (artist Patricia Nguyen + designer John Lee) selected April 2019; years of delay and zero city funding (ProPublica 2020) — then **GROUNDBREAKING July 8, 2026**, at 5520 S. Martin Luther King Drive (Washington Park), Mayor Brandon Johnson presiding; total cost now **$4.7M** ($1M city funds, lots sold for $1, Mellon Foundation support); **expected completion 2027** — eleven years after the promise. Johnson (verbatim): "The city of Chicago — we are grateful for your survival. But we deeply, deeply apologize…" Date-stamp: as of July 2026.
- **BU-35 (H)** Burge **died September 19, 2018**, age 70, in Florida — still drawing his police pension, having never admitted anything. ⚠ Fahey/O'Brien and every murder victim in underlying cases: dignity; no gore.
- **BU-36 (H · date-stamped)** **The money:** best sourced tally — **Injustice Watch (June 2022): more than $210 million and counting** (settlements/judgments/reparations $108.2M + pensions to accused torturers $38.7M + city outside counsel $37.5M + county lawyers $19.5M + TIRC/Court of Claims $7.9M). - **BU-37 (H · ⚠ per-person status care)** **Exonerations continue after his death:** Jackie Wilson (Andrew's brother — new trial 2018, **certificate of innocence Dec 2020**); Kevin Bailey & Corey Batchelor (exonerated 2018, $14M combined 2022); James Gibson (vacated 2019); a 2022 mass hearing overturned eight torture-linked murder convictions; Robert Henderson & Sean Tyler (certificates of innocence **2024**); Tyrece Williams (**2025**). ~**20 survivors remained imprisoned** at reparations time (advocate figure, M — hedge "roughly twenty"). ## 🚫 SCRIPT GUARDRAILS

- **Survivor legal status discipline (⚠ LIVING):** Patterson, Hobley, Orange, Howard = pardoned on innocence grounds → innocence statable. Kitchen = exonerated + certificate of innocence → statable. Jackie Wilson = certificate of innocence → statable. **Andrew Wilson was guilty of the Fahey/O'Brien murders** — the film says so plainly; his torture is the point, not his innocence. All other claimants: state the TORTURE finding, never blanket innocence.
- **Midnight Crew detectives:** name NO individual detective as a torturer except where quoting an official finding; default to "his men," "his detectives." (Yucaitis/O'Hara may be named for the adjudicated Police Board outcome only — suspended, returned to duty.)

- **Torture is NEVER depicted** (visuals): objects, shadows, empty rooms, aftermath; no restrained person, no device touching anyone, no distress faces, no screams in sound design. Ad-safety MEDIUM ceiling.
- **Race:** the victims were almost all Black men; register is sober; silhouettes are dignified; no lurid framing.
- **Hedged figures:** "more than 100 / at least 118 by his victims' lawyers' count"; "about $7 million" (Egan/Boyle cost); "roughly $3,000 a month" (pension); "roughly twenty still inside"; "more than $210 million by 2022 — and counting." **Exact of record (no hedge needed):** 4½ years; 3 counts; June 28 2010; Jan 21 2011; 4–4; May 6 2015; $5.5M/57; Sept 19 2018.
- **Daley:** state ONLY the documented chain (Brzeczek's Feb 25, 1982 letter; no response; the 2006 report faulted him). No speculation about motive.
