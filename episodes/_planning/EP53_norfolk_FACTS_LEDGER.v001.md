**HARD FRAME:** The four sailors — **Danial Williams, Joseph J. Dick Jr., Derek Tice, Eric Wilson — are LIVING, their convictions VACATED (2011/2016), and they hold ABSOLUTE PARDONS (2017)**. A federal judge found them **actually innocent**. Their innocence is stated as FACT. Two adjudicated villains may be named plainly within the record: **Omar Ballard** (sole DNA match; pleaded guilty 2000; has always said he acted alone) and **Det. Robert Glenn Ford** (obtained the false confessions; later convicted in federal court of extortion and lying to the FBI — in unrelated cases — and sentenced to 12½ years). **Michelle Moore-Bosko was a real 18-year-old murder victim: the rape is NEVER depicted or dwelt on — verbal, restrained, off-screen.** Her parents believed the four guilty for years — **handled with dignity, never vilified.**

### The crime & the victim (Act I)
- **NF-01 (H · ⚠)** Victim: **Michelle Moore-Bosko, 18**, wife of a US Navy sailor, killed in her **Norfolk, Virginia apartment** — she was **raped, stabbed, and strangled**. Src: Frontline timeline; Tice v. Johnson (4th Cir.); Wikipedia. Use: name her with dignity; the assault is stated in ONE restrained clause, never described further, never depicted.
- **NF-02 (H)** Date: the attack occurred the **night of July 7–8, 1997**; the body was **discovered July 8, 1997**. Src: Frontline timeline ("July 7"); 4th Cir. ("July 8"). CONFLICT = which side of midnight. Use the safe phrasing: "the night of July 7–8, 1997" / "found on July 8."
- **NF-03 (H)** Her husband, **William "Billy" Bosko** (Navy sailor, ~19), **found her body on July 8, 1997, returning from a week at sea**. Src: Frontline timeline; NRE; Wikipedia. Note: his ship's name NOT verified — do not name it. ⚠ Handle Billy with dignity.
- **NF-04 (M)** Apartment complex name reported as "Bayshore Gardens." Single source (Wikipedia). Use: say "an apartment complex near the Norfolk naval base" — do NOT use the name.
- **NF-05 (H · ⚠)** Wounds: death by **manual strangulation plus multiple stab wounds**; Gibney's opinion: the wounds were **"of uniform depth and closely bunched together, indicating that a single individual inflicted them."** Exact count varies by source (17 vs 20) — **state NO number**. Src: Williams v. Brown (vlex); Tice v. Johnson. Use: only as the single-attacker forensic point, one clause, clinical.
- **NF-06 (H)** The crime scene indicated a **single attacker**: small apartment, **no sign of forced entry**, scene consistent with one offender; the only DNA recovered (vaginal swabs, fingernail scrapings, blanket) was later shown to belong to **Omar Ballard alone**; all seven charged sailors were **flatly excluded** by DNA. Src: Tice v. Johnson; Williams v. Brown. Use: load-bearing spine of the film; can be stated flatly.

### The first confession — Danial Williams (Act I)
- **NF-07 (H)** **Danial Williams**, 25, Navy sailor, **no criminal record**, lived in the same building with his wife and his roommate **Joseph Dick**. He became the suspect **within hours** after Michelle's friend and neighbor **Tamika Taylor** told police Williams seemed obsessed with / fixated on Michelle. Src: NRE summary; Wikipedia. Use: OK; frame Taylor neutrally (a grieving friend answering questions — not a villain).
- **NF-08 (H)** Williams was interrogated **overnight July 8–9, 1997, roughly 11 hours** (Frontline: "11+ hours," Detectives Evans and Ford). **Det. Robert Glenn Ford took over partway through** (commonly reported ~8 hours in); Williams was **falsely told he had failed a polygraph**; he **confessed to Ford before dawn on July 9, 1997**, and was arrested. Src: Frontline timeline; NRE; Wikipedia. CONFLICT on internal hour-by-hour timing → safe phrasing: "after roughly eleven hours overnight, Ford took over, and Williams confessed before dawn."
- **NF-09 (H pattern / M detail)** Williams' confession **got key facts wrong and was corrected across multiple statements**; Gibney: police **"took multiple statements of the men to groom them to say what the police wanted."** (The specific first-version detail that he claimed he beat her with a shoe = M — soft-cite or omit.) Src: Williams v. Brown; Frontline. Use: the grooming finding is court-established; attribute wrong-detail specifics generally ("his first account got the method of the murder wrong; the details were corrected as the interrogation went on").
- **NF-10 (M-H · ⚠)** Williams' wife **Nicole was dying of ovarian cancer** during this period; she died **November 2, 1997**, about four months after his arrest. Src: Wikipedia (date); the cancer is also in Frontline's film. Use: one restrained beat; hedge the date ("that autumn").
- **NF-11 (H)** Interrogation methods across the four, as characterized by Frontline and court findings: **threats of the death penalty, sleep deprivation, intimidation, false polygraph claims, and feeding/correcting crime-scene facts**. Src: Frontline; Williams v. Brown. Use: attribute ("as a federal judge later found" / "as Frontline reported").

- **NF-15 (H)** **Derek Tice** was **arrested June 18, 1998** (in Florida) and confessed after a long interrogation (Frontline: 11 hours; his signed statement rendered June 25, 1998 in the 4th Cir. account — CONFLICT on the day → say "after his own marathon interrogation"). - **NF-19 (H)** **Ballard knew Michelle.** 4th Cir.: "Ballard and Michelle were friends, and … Ballard frequently visited Michelle in her apartment"; he was an acquaintance of hers and Tamika Taylor's. Src: Tice v. Johnson. Use: state plainly; no lurid embellishment.

Src: Tice v. Johnson; Wikipedia. Use: hedge exact dates ("days before… days after"); NEVER name or characterize the 14-year-old beyond this clause.

Src: Tice v. Johnson (FindLaw text); Frontline timeline. ### The four outcomes (Act III close)
- **NF-27 (H)** **Danial Williams pleaded guilty January 22, 1999** — capital murder and rape, to avoid the death penalty — **life without parole** (Frontline: two life sentences). NOTE THE ORDER: his plea came **one month BEFORE Ballard's letter surfaced**. Src: Frontline timeline. Use: the plea-under-death-threat logic; date is load-bearing for the tragedy.
- **NF-28 (H)** **Joseph Dick pleaded guilty April 21, 1999** — **AFTER Ballard's DNA match (March 4, 1999)** — sentenced September 8, 1999 to **two life terms**; he **testified for the prosecution** against Wilson and Tice (by then he believed, or performed belief in, the story that had been built for him). Src: Frontline timeline; Wikipedia. Use: state the date order explicitly — the system took a plea from a DNA-excluded man AFTER it had the real killer's match.
- **NF-29 (H)** **Eric Wilson**, tried June 1999: on **June 16, 1999** the jury **convicted him of rape but ACQUITTED him of murder** — **8.5 years**. Released **September 2005** (~7.4 years from his April 1998 arrest). He then had to **register as a sex offender** — and was **barred from adopting his stepson** — until the 2017 pardon. Src: Frontline timeline; Time archive; AP (Fox print). Use: the "smallest" sentence and its long tail; the stepson detail is H (AP) and lands the human cost.
- **NF-30 (H)** **Derek Tice**: convicted **February 2000** (exact day conflicts: Feb 8 vs 14 — say "February 2000") of capital murder and rape — **two life sentences**; conviction **reversed on appeal 2002**; **retried and convicted again January 31, 2003** — two life sentences again. Src: Frontline timeline; Tice v. Johnson. Use: plainly.
- **NF-31 (H approximations)** Years served (arrest → release): **Williams ~12** (Jul 1997–Aug 2009), **Dick ~11.5** (Jan 1998–Aug 2009), **Tice ~11** (Jun 1998–Aug/Sep 2009), **Wilson ~7.5** (Apr 1998–Sep 2005). Combined ≈ **42 person-years**. Src: computed from H-confidence dates above. Use: hedge ("about," "nearly"); "combined, more than forty years" is safe.

- **NF-39 (H · VERBATIM-SAFE)** **September 2016** (opinion dated Sept 26, 2016 per the published text; secondary reports vary → say "in September 2016"): **U.S. District Judge John A. - **NF-41 (H)** **March 21, 2017:** Gov. **Terry McAuliffe** granted **ABSOLUTE (full) pardons to all four**, including Wilson — clearing their records and ending sex-offender registration. Src: WAVY & WTKR investigative reports; Frontline. Use: one devastating beat — the pattern was known. (Exact year he joined homicide NOT verified — don't state.)
- **NF-44 (H)** Ford's federal case: **indicted May 7, 2010; convicted October 27, 2010** of **conspiracy to commit extortion under color of official right, extortion, and making false statements to the FBI** (DOJ/FBI releases; R3 correction 2026-07-26 — the prior "two counts of extortion" mislabeled the conspiracy count; narration-safe phrasing: "extortion, under color of his badge" + "lied to the FBI") — he **took payments from criminal defendants in exchange for favorable treatment**, in cases **UNRELATED to the Norfolk Four**; **sentenced February 25, 2011 to 12 years 6 months** in federal prison (E.D.
