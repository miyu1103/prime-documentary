## What v003 changes about how the film ends

The film's controlling idea — *a rule written for events with organisers cannot see a crowd that has
none* — can now be closed **on the text of the rule itself**, in three dated sentences and nothing
else:

1. **29 October 2022.** No provision required anyone to plan for a crowd with no organiser.
2. **30 September 2024.** A court acquitted the district officials, in terms, because of that
   (IT-34, IT-34a).
3. **26 December 2023.** The provision exists. It is Article 66-11, paragraph 1, and it names the
   head of the local government (IT-78, IT-79).

Note the order of the dates: **the rule was already law when the court delivered the acquittal.** The
court was applying the law as it stood in 2022, and the film must say so plainly rather than letting
the chronology imply that a court ignored a rule that existed. That is the single most likely
misreading of this material and ⛔-04 already forbids it.

And the last beat is IT-82: three and a half years on, the duty exists and the penalty for ignoring
it was still a bill.
