- **RS-10 (A — mechanism, uncontested)** **What a foreclosure affidavit is.** In a judicial foreclosure the servicer must file a sworn statement in which a named individual attests, on penalty of perjury, to **personal knowledge** of the loan, the ownership of the note, the amount owed and the default. Most homeowners in foreclosure are unrepresented and many never appear, so in a great many cases the affidavit is the only evidence the court receives. This is stated in the film as **one person's obligation to read**, never as a doctrine block (R-2).
