# EP60 Surfside — thumbnail face plate (v001)

## Why

Every other upcoming episode has a purpose-made emotive close-up under
`H:\pd-media\assets\ai\<slug>\thumb\<SLUG>_FACE_v001.png`, which
`scripts/build_thumbs_ctr_v2.py --face` crops and types over. **EP60 has none**, and the
thumbnail gate refuses to ship without a legible subject.

## Save as

    H:\pd-media\assets\ai\surfside\thumb\SURFSIDE_FACE_v001.png

## Format — this part is load-bearing

- **1920×1080, 16:9.** The builder crops to 1280×720 and frames the face at roughly 55% of
  frame height with the eyes on the upper third.
- **The subject occupies ONE side only — put them on the LEFT.** The opposite side must be
  large, dark and near-empty: that is where the two-line headline sits on its own plate. If
  the subject is centred, the type lands on their face.
- **No text, no signage, no logo anywhere in frame.**
- **No real-person likeness.** Nobody in this image may resemble a real resident, official,
  engineer or family member. Generic person only.

## The prompt

A man in his fifties standing at night in the open air, seen in close-up from the chest up on
the left of the frame, lit only by a hard white work light from the right that rakes across one
side of his face and leaves the other in shadow. Fine pale dust settles on his hair and the
shoulders of a plain dark jacket. He is looking slightly up and past the camera at something out
of frame, mouth closed, jaw set — the expression of a man being told a number he already knew.
Behind him the background falls away into deep near-black with a faint haze of suspended dust
catching the light, no building, no rubble, no vehicle legible. Cinematic still, somber
documentary grade, cool white work-light key against warm sodium spill far behind, shallow depth
of field, telephoto compression, ultra-detailed, photoreal, 4K, 16:9, fine film grain.
Avoid: text, words, letters, numbers, captions, watermark, logo, readable sign, news chyron,
broadcaster mark, real celebrity, recognizable real person, identifiable public figure, portrait
of a specific individual, uniform insignia, badge, rescue-service branding, blood, injury, gore,
corpse, body, collapsed figure, weeping face, screaming face, children, subject centred in frame,
subject on the right, bright background, busy background, second person in frame.

## If the first attempt centres the subject

Re-run with `standing on the far left of the frame, the entire right half of the image empty and
in deep shadow` added to the opening sentence. The empty right half is not decoration — it is
where the words go.
