## WHY 60 MINUTES IS EARNED (the retention thesis)
This is not a single-scene SCOTUS case — it is a **13-year human saga** with a built-in reversal (children convicted on coerced confessions → the real attacker's DNA undoes everything). The length is justified ONLY if every act ends on an **open loop** and the five are drawn as **individuals**, not a headline. Channel data says long-form under-retains (see SCALE_NOTE); we buy the hour back with per-act hooks, chaptered timestamps, and front-loading the interrogation act (the moral engine) early.
