## 1. THE STRUCTURE — what was built, and where

| id | fact | grade | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| MO-01 | The Viadotto Polcevera stands at **km 000+551 of the A10 Genova–Savona motorway**, in the comune of Genova | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 p.7 |
| MO-02 | It was **the only cable-stayed viaduct on the entire ASPI network** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 p.7 |
| MO-03 | Designed by **Prof. Ing. Riccardo Morandi**; the design dates from **1962**, construction began in **1963**, and it was **inaugurated in 1967** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 pp.7, 21 |

| MO-06 | The deck is **just over eighteen metres wide** and runs on **11 spans with clear spans varying from 65 m to 208 m** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 pp.8, 21 |
| MO-07 | It crosses the Polcevera valley in **a densely urbanised and industrialised area, with buildings, industrial plants, a railway yard, railway lines and urban streets** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 pp.7–8 |
| MO-08 | **Pier 9 — the one that collapsed — stands at about km 0+470** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 p.21 |
| MO-09 | Three of the spans use the **"sistema bilanciato"** (piers 9, 10 and 11): a continuous multi-cell box girder **171 m long** (piers 9 and 10; **145 m** at pier 11), carried on four struts and then on **four tie-members of prestressed reinforced concrete, called *stralli*,** running from the tops of antennae standing **up to 90 metres above the valley floor** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 pp.8, 22 |
| MO-10 | The rest of the deck to the west uses **six V-shaped trestles in ordinary reinforced concrete** and one vertical pier at the west end | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 p.8 |
| MO-11 | Between the box-girder decks sit **"impalcati tampone" — 36-metre infill decks of six prestressed concrete beams** joined by cross-beams and a top slab | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 pp.8, 24 |
| MO-12 | **The viaduct has ten of these infill decks** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 p.76 |
| MO-13 | The balanced systems repeat the structural type Morandi had used a few years earlier for the **Ponte General Rafael Urdaneta** at Maracaibo, Venezuela, and used again at the **Wadi al-Kuf bridge** in Libya | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 p.23 |
| MO-14 | The infill decks overhung **industrial buildings, railway lines and roads**; the balanced systems stood **a few metres from inhabited buildings housing at least 500 people**; the collapse of one deck would put the whole viaduct out of use — a nodal point for two motorways, the port of Genoa and the adjacent industries | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 p.75 n.13 |

## 2. THE STAYS — why there was concrete around the steel

**This section is the film's central object. Every row here is primary.**

| id | fact | grade | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| MO-15 | The *stralli* are made of **strands of high-tensile steel**, passing over the antenna on a special saddle of steel plate and sections embedded in the concrete pour | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 p.23 |
| MO-16 | Each stay carries **primary cables: 16 cables of 16 strands plus 8 cables of 12 strands — 352 strands in all**, each strand of half-inch (12.7 mm) nominal diameter | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 p.23 |
| MO-17 | And **secondary cables: 28 cables of 4 strands — 112 strands**, of the same half-inch diameter | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 p.23 |
| MO-18 | The primary cables carry, in the main, the dead weight of the deck; once the deck was built they were tensioned to balance the vertical forces from the weight of the box girder | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 p.23 |
| MO-19 | **Only then was the concrete sheath built** — of precast segments, **outside the primary cables and, at first, free to slide against them** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 p.24 |
| MO-20 | The secondary cables sit inside that sheath, further out than the primary cables, and are tensioned so as to **put the sheath itself into compression** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 p.24 |
| MO-21 | Finally the whole system — primary cables, secondary cables and concrete sheath — **was made monolithic by injecting grout into the ducts** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 p.24 |
| MO-22 | The stated purpose of that arrangement was **"the advantage of preserving the metal part of the stays from corrosion"**, while increasing stiffness and limiting elongation under variable load | VERBATIM QUOTE | SRC-0001 p.24 |
| MO-23 | It is universally accepted that **indirect investigation methods do not permit quantitative assessment; direct investigation is therefore indispensable** | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 p.60 |

## ⛔ QUARANTINE — assertions that must never appear

| id | rule |
|---|---|
| ⛔-01 | **Never state or imply that any named individual caused the collapse.** Where a convicted person is named, **"convicted at first instance"** and **"the judgment is not final"** appear in the same breath. Where an acquitted person is named — **Roberto Ferrazza** above all — **the acquittal appears in the same breath.** This binds the title, the thumbnail text, the description and every figure card |
| ⛔-02 | **Never assert intent or foresight.** Not "they knew it would collapse", not "deliberately", not "they let it fall". The court excluded article 61 no. 3 for every defendant and acquitted all of them of articles 432 and 437 "because the fact does not exist" (MO-119, MO-124). The convictions are for negligence |
| ⛔-03 | **Never say Morandi predicted this collapse.** The permitted form is: Aspi asked him for a report in 1981, and from the 1980s onward the bridge was the subject of a series of maintenance interventions (MO-30, AB-04) |
| ⛔-04 | **Never give a single cause as settled.** The form is always: the ministry's commission concluded X in September 2018; the court's experts concluded Y in December 2020; the commission described its own account as plausible but not definitive (MO-100, MO-102, MO-108, AB-03) |
| ⛔-05 | **No victim is named, shown, aged, or characterised.** 43 people died and 13 were injured; the film names none of them, shows no photograph, no memorial bearing faces, no funeral, no family |
| ⛔-06 | **Never depict the fall.** No falling vehicle, no vehicle at the broken edge, no wreckage implying a person inside, no rescue, no body, no stretcher. The collapse is shown as **the gap afterwards, as scale, and as a severed road with a barrier across it** |
| ⛔-07 | **Never blame the inspector.** The scoring rows (MO-51 to MO-60) are evidence about a system, not about the person holding the clipboard. No line, caption or image attributes a score to a named or depicted individual, and the film does not invent an inspector's thoughts |
| ⛔-08 | **Never say the storm, the lightning or a lorry's weight caused it.** None of these is a finding in anything retrieved |
| ⛔-09 | **Never say Autostrade per l'Italia or Spea were convicted.** The operative part read for this film disposes of natural persons only (AB-05) |
| ⛔-10 | **Never present the new bridge as consolation, closure or an answer.** State what was built (MO-138 to MO-141) and, specifically, **do not repeat the 43-lamps story**: the 43 light points were in the original design and the number was reduced (MO-141) |
| ⛔-11 | **No figure — deaths, injuries, metres, years, euros, percentages, scores, sentences — that is not a row above.** Where a figure is a range, the range is spoken; it is never collapsed to one number |
| ⛔-12 | **No generated image may carry legible Italian text that reads as a document, a report, a score sheet, a newspaper or a judgment.** Typography that must be read is composited in Remotion over a blank ground. Italian lettering in the world of the film is present but **unreadable** |
| ⛔-13 | **The 2009 handwritten note (MO-113) is SECONDARY.** It may be referred to as reported, with its source named on screen, and it may not carry an act's turn or the hook |
| ⛔-14 | **The 1993 corrosion percentages and the 2017 project's 10–20 % estimate (MO-110, MO-112) are SECONDARY.** They may be narrated as what the court's experts found, attributed in the same sentence, and they may not stand as the film's own statement of the bridge's condition |

## The one-sentence contradiction this film is built on

> The rule that said close the road was written by the company that would have had to close it; the
> assessment that would have said whether the bridge was safe was owed by law in 2013, was reported
> as done in 2017, had never been made — and the whole of it was legal until the moment the deck
> came down.

Every act must serve that sentence, and ⛔-01 governs how the people in it are named.
