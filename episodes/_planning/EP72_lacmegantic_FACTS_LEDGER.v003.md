## 15. WHAT THE COUNTRY CHANGED

| id | fact | grade | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| LM-89 | On **23 July 2013 — seventeen days after the derailment** — Transport Canada issued an **emergency directive** requiring: **at least two persons** to operate trains carrying tank cars of dangerous goods; **no dangerous-goods train left unattended on a main track**; cabs of unattended trains **locked and the reverser handles removed**; hand-brake requirements for trains left unattended **more than an hour**; and **both** the automatic and the independent brake applied at maximum force for trains left an hour or less | VERBATIM | SRC-0017 |
| LM-90 | The emergency directives took effect immediately and ran until December | VERBATIM | SRC-0017 |
| LM-91 | In **July 2015** Transport Canada approved revisions to **Rule 112 of the Canadian Railway Operating Rules**, formalising those provisions, making them **permanent** and more detailed | VERBATIM | SRC-0017 |

## What v003 changes about the film

1. **ACT_3 gets its morning.** The film currently ends the night at the derailment. It took until the
   following afternoon to contain the fire (LM-83), two thousand people — a third of the town — were
   out of their homes (LM-81), and forty buildings burned (LM-84).
2. **ACT_5 gets the rule that changed.** Seventeen days later the regulator required two people on a
   dangerous-goods train and banned leaving one unattended on a main track (LM-89). Under ⛔-13 the
   film states this **as a response**, immediately after the passage that says the investigation
   could not conclude a second person would have changed this night. The two facts are allowed to sit
   next to each other. They are not allowed to be joined.
3. **The ending gets its last image.** Ten years on, freight trains were still running through the
   town (LM-95); the bypass is funded, expropriated and signed (LM-96–LM-98); and the village where
   the train was parked has withdrawn its support for it (LM-99). The empty chair can now be followed
   by a line that is a fact rather than a flourish.
