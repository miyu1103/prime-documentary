## 0. Executive summary

1. Retention is decided in **0–120 seconds**: median half-life of our 19 real curves is **42s**; 68% of viewers are gone by 2:00. Everything after 2:00 decays gently in good builds (1.0–1.4pt/min) — the war is the opening.
2. The three measured leaks, in order: (a) first 8s content loses to peers (12/19 videos below peer median by 6–7s), (b) the **brand-OP/thesis transition at 11–43s**, (c) an **explanation block at 80–180s** (12/19 cliffs; verified against film.json: legislative-history/procedure blocks, not the OP itself).
3. The winning opening is externally unanimous (20/26 winners) and internally validated (Titan 0.949 @30s): **cold open with VO ≤1.5s, declarative person+specific+incongruity first sentence, loop set as a BUT-contradiction by ~0:32, brand sting ≤5s AFTER the loop, post-brand line escalates**.
4. "Biggest reveal last" is corrected to a **reveal LADDER**: shock at 0% (cut pre-resolution), mid reveal ~50%, primary reveal starts 65–85% and resolves by 92%; nothing new after 92%.
5. Craft gap is systematic and fixable: we **command emotions** (9–14 imperatives/script vs winners' 0), run one register for 30 minutes, and let specificity lapse for 129s stretches. 26-point checklist now exists; EP52/EP53 fail only the D-section + specificity checks.
6. Distribution: **views are Shorts (65%, 0.16 min/view); watch time is Suggested (44.5%) and 100% of it is borrowed from other channels' videos**. Our own session loop (end screens, playlists, long-form pinned comments, interlinks) is entirely unbuilt; PLAYLIST already measures 19.6 min/view. Cheapest big lever on the board.
7. Audience is NOT the genre's: **93.4% male, 90.9% aged 55+** (signed-in sample, directional) — an older-male civics/overreach audience, not female-25–54 true crime. Frame wrongful conviction as "the system failed a man", never whodunit. GB/AU/NL retain 2.4–2.8× better than US (tiny n → pilot, don't pivot).
8. Packaging grammar from the outlier study: **stakes-gap numbers on both ends** ("Civil infraction → 7–15 years", 68× at 24k subs), **present-tense injustice + the official's own damning quote** — never resolved-form ("Exonerated after 30 years") packaging. 30min+ is breakout-compatible at every channel size; premise, not length, is the gate.
9. Master ranking (§9): top levers are the own-session machine (end screens + playlists), the 0:32 cold-open/≤5s-sting retrofit, the 80–180s fold-in rule, and stakes-gap titling — all cheap, all measured.

## R. RECONCILIATIONS — tensions resolved explicitly

### R-a. T7 "present-tense injustice" vs the playbook's time-jump title grammar
**Tension:** CTR_PLAYBOOK v002 §2 measured "N years later" as the single biggest long-form title over-indexer (9.0×), and the theme thread inherits that grammar. T7's corpus scan found the innocence lane's ceiling: **"exonerated after N years" packaging never exceeds ~6×**, while present-tense injustice ("They knew I was innocent — you're still going to jail") is the form that breaks out.
**Resolution (both are true; they measure different things):** "N years later" wins as a *forward tension* ingredient (something is about to surface); "after N years" loses as *resolved relief* (tension already spent). The synthesis:
- **Titles/thumbs: stakes-gap numbers > time-jump.** First choice is the stakes gap with numbers on both ends ("A $2 fine. They took her house."). Time may appear only as a stake or a fuse ("She followed the rules for 38 years. Then the IRS took everything." — playbook's own rewrite already obeys this), **never as the resolution** ("Freed/Exonerated/Overturned after N years" is banned packaging).
- **The time-jump belongs inside the film** — it is the mid/primary reveal engine (bandana 25 years later), not the package. Package sells the wrong-in-progress; the film delivers the jump.
- No silent contradiction with the playbook: §2's over-index list stands; this adds a *polarity constraint* on how "N years" is phrased. Feed to the 8/8 re-measure as an A/B (§8-E3).

### R-b. T3 reveal ladder vs our "biggest reveal last" doctrine
**Corrected doctrine:** the payoff is a **ladder, not a stacked bomb**. (i) The cold open shows the story's single most dramatic moment at 0%, cut before resolution (Cooper's jump). (ii) A mid-size reveal lands ~50%. (iii) The primary reveal **starts at 65–85% and resolves by 92%**; the final act may be a ladder of 3–5 sub-payoffs (Cooper's suspects). (iv) **No new core fact after 92%** — the last 5–10% is falling action; the only thing allowed late (≤90%) is a *reframe* of already-shown facts. "Reveal stacked last" survives only as "primary reveal in the final third"; anything essential parked in the final minutes plays to the smallest surviving audience. T4 confirms compatibility: winners trail the *existence* of the ending early (MrBallen's envelopes) and pay it off late — tease existence, withhold content.

### R-c. T2 brand-sting ≤5s vs our current gold BrandOpening
**Tension:** current canon is cold open ~22s → gold BrandOpening (≈10s-class, full musical beat) → thesis OPENING. T2: winners' stings are 4–15s, median position 45–60s, always after the loop, music continuous; the biggest lane channels use **no** front card at all. Our own measured drops cluster at **11–24s** and Titan bleeds 26.5pt at **43s** — exactly the brand/thesis transition.
**Resolution:** keep the brand moment (franchise recognition) but **≤5 seconds, audio-continuous, fused with the ritual title line ("This is the case of ___"), placed at ~0:32 — after the BUT-loop exists, never before**. The OP/ED-taste directive ([[feedback-opening-ending-taste]]) is preserved: same gold visual language, cut to a 5s version; this is a trim, not a redesign. Verify with the next curve pull: drop at the sting boundary must be <15pt.

### R-d. T1 "no explanation block at 80–180s" vs our post-brand OPENING thesis paragraphs
**Tension:** canon structure inserts a thesis OPENING after the brand card; T1's curves put 12/19 videos' biggest post-minute cliff at 80–180s, and film.json cross-checks show the cliff is the **context/history explanation block** (Bank Secrecy Act history at 65–93s in hinders, "completely routine" procedure in rodriguez). CTR_PLAYBOOK §3.2 already ordered "delete the 0:30–1:15 thesis block" — T1 confirms it with curves and extends the danger zone to 180s.
**Resolution:** the post-brand OPENING is reduced to **one escalating sentence** (a new concrete or an evidence-promise), then a date/place anchor drops us into Act 1. All doctrine/legislative/context exposition is **folded in one sentence at a time after the first payoff**; a contiguous ≥20s person-action-free explanation anywhere in 60–180s is a build FAIL. Thesis paragraphs as a block are retired.

### R-e. T6 93% male / 91% 55+ vs "broader true-crime audience" assumptions
**Tension:** genre research (Pew/YouGov) says true crime skews **female 25–54**; several packaging instincts (emotional victim-POV, whodunit framing, youth-coded pacing) implicitly assume that audience. Our measured audience is the opposite: **93.4% male, 90.9% 55+**, shares by email, 6% TV device share — a civics/"government overreach" audience recruited by our actual register (court-record, no gore).
**Resolution:** we are a **legal/finance-documentary channel with true-crime skin, not a mainstream true-crime channel**. Topic and tone optimize for older American men: state power, property, police, institutional betrayal; wrongful conviction framed as "the system failed a man." Do not chase the female genre audience — it would require sensational packaging that clashes with the voice that recruited the audience we have.
**Sample-size honesty:** the demographic split is from signed-in viewers on a ~3.3k-view channel — possibly a few hundred effective views; directional, extreme enough to act on, **re-pull after the next 10k views before any irreversible brand decision**. GB/CA/NL/AU AVPs rest on 22–110 views each — that justifies exactly one UK pilot episode, not a pivot.

### R-f. (bonus) T1/T7 "length is fine" vs standing memory "kill 27–36min, stay 12min"
The 7/03 verdict ("長尺化はデータ否定") was a **views-basis** call and remains true at the entrance (long-form CTR 1.2–2.4% vs 2.4–4.5%). On a **watch-minutes basis** (the algorithm's fuel) it inverts: AVP is flat across bands (~19%) and 23–36min delivers 3.2× minutes/view; T7 adds that 5/8 breakouts are ≥36min on channels of every size. Corrected rule: **length is earned by narrative density, not banned** — walk runtime up only for character/turn-rich stories that can hold decay ≤1.5pt/min, keep doctrine-explainers at 11–12min, and treat long-form CTR <1% as a packaging emergency, not a length verdict. (n is small; do not hard-gate.)

## 1. RETENTION (T1 — real curves, 19/49 long-forms, official Analytics API)

**Evidence:** median residuals 30s=62% / 1min=40% / 2min=32% / 4min=22% / 8min=18%; half-life median **42s** (range 17–164s). 12/19 videos below peer median (rel_perf<0.5) by 6–7s. 12/19 biggest post-minute cliffs at **80–180s** = explanation blocks (film.json cross-check). Good builds decay 1.0–1.4pt/min after 2:00, bad ones 2–5. AWT≥AVD ×1.0–4.5 (Shorts-feed contamination: 0.16 min/view vs RELATED 3.9). florence: half-life 17s + rising curve = packaging/opening mismatch signature. Cotton's "This is the story of…" preamble halved the audience by 28s vs glover's scene-built cold open.

**Finding:** the opening 2 minutes are the entire game; mid-video is already healthy in new-recipe builds; lifetime AVD/AVP is an unusable quality metric on this channel.

**Rules:**
- **R-1 [MUST]** Retention verdicts use ① real curves (`yt_studio_retention.py --source api`, weekly-capable) and ② AWT-from-impressions. Lifetime AVD/AVP alone is banned as a quality judgment (Shorts contamination measured).
- **R-2 [MUST]** No explanation block at 60–180s: no contiguous ≥20s of person-action-free exposition (legislative history, procedure, doctrine) before the first story payoff. Fold context in one sentence at a time. Gate: window-scoped detector in `check_retention_cadence.py`.
- **R-3 [MUST]** 0–120s battlefield: a new-information beat every ≤45s in the first 2 minutes; the story's most important fact/emotional core lands by 90s — no saving it for later. (Broader ≤150s re-hook cadence continues after 2:00, per R-15.)
- **R-4 [SHOULD]** Post-publish decay gate: at day 7, if the 2:00→80% segment decays >1.5pt/min, the section enters retro (pd-retro). Measured floor from good builds (1.0–1.4), inference as a gate.
- **R-5 [SHOULD]** Length policy per reconciliation R-f (density-earned, CTR-guarded).
- **R-6 [MUST]** Packaging⇔opening promise: the thumbnail's scene is the literal first shot and the thumb text is spoken/shown within 20s (playbook §3 + florence signature: half-life <20s with a rising 2-min curve = mismatch alarm, monitor for it).

## 2. HOOK / FIRST 30 SECONDS (T2 — 29 winner openings transcribed, 4 losers, cross-checked on our curves)

**Evidence:** 20/26 verified winners: case content inside 5s; **zero** winners open with >5s brand animation or host-first; 0/26 open with a question; median subject named ~20s; loops are BUT-contradictions ("He confessed. But were they telling the truth?"); stings 4–15s at 45–60s, music continuous; loser signature = promo/sponsor/no-named-human up front. Ours: Titan cold open 0.949 @30s (best), concept-led opens 0.47–0.48 (worst); drops cluster 11–24s and 43s = brand/thesis transitions; every measured long fails rel_perf_30s ≥0.5 → binding constraint.

**Finding:** our cold-open bet is right; the leak is the hand-off (sting too long/too early) and first-sentence strength. Verdict: **KEEP cold open, ADJUST 4** (lengthen to ~30–32s so the loop precedes the sting; shrink sting to ≤5s fused with title line; person before principle; post-brand escalates, never restates).

**Rules (the Opening Formula, 0:00–0:45):**
- **R-7 [MUST]** Sound (VO or diegetic case audio) within 1.5s; first frame is case-specific; no dead air >1.5s in the first 45s; no brand-first, no host-first.
- **R-8 [MUST]** First sentence: declarative, containing a **person + hard specific (number/date/place/source) + incongruity**. Never a question; no "This is the story of…"; no thesis/abstraction before a named human (Swartz/cotton rule — gate: abstraction-class vocab without an event verb in the first two sentences = FAIL).
- **R-9 [MUST]** Protagonist/victim named by ~0:15; crime/loss stated and the opposing force (police/IRS/court) planted by 0:28. Real primary audio (911/hearing tape) beats narration — play 3–6s if held.
- **R-10 [MUST]** Open loop = **BUT-contradiction** (established fact + reversal), planted by ~0:32, **before** the sting. A topic-question is not a loop. Optional +1 evidence-promise (a concrete artifact, "the memo the jury never saw") — never "by the end of this video".
- **R-11 [MUST]** Brand sting ≤5s, audio-continuous, fused with the ritual title line; placed only after the loop exists (~0:32–0:37). (Reconciliation R-c.)
- **R-12 [MUST]** Post-brand (0:37–0:45): ONE new escalating concrete, then date/place anchor into Act 1. No restatement, no "in this video we will". (Reconciliation R-d.)
- **R-13 [MUST]** First-45s bans: subscribe/bell/sponsor/channel self-description/"welcome to"; two consecutive sentences without a new concrete fact; brand animation >5s; music-only gap >2s. Cadence: one new concrete every 3–8s.
- Reveal economy: tease the outcome's **existence**, never its content (feeds R-14).

## 3. STRUCTURE & PACING (T3 — 17 winners, 3 tier-A anchor dissections, our Titan-vs-Swartz contrast)

**Evidence:** Cooper (tier-A chapter map): acts 3–6min, ≤21% of runtime each, suspects-ladder occupies 65–97%; JCS 26min: no chapters, micro-analysis re-hook every 30–90s, shock beats mid-video, comparison-case reset in the back third; Man in Cave 74min: part cards, known-outcome dread, climax 75–85%, tonal breathers after heavy sections. Ours: Titan (chaptered chronological disaster, AWT 8:15) vs Swartz (essay-led, AWT 1:09 — the median clicker never met Act 2); story-first films hold 19–24% AVP, concept-first 4–15%.

**Finding:** per-runtime templates (below) + the reveal-ladder correction (R-b). Narrative beats essay in this lane, structurally and terminally.

**Rules:**
- **R-14 [MUST]** Reveal-placement gate: cold open = peak moment at 0% cut pre-resolution; mid reveal ~45–60%; `primary_reveal` starts 65–85% and resolves by 92%; no `new_fact_core` after 92%; `cold_open_callback` beat required at 70–90%.
- **R-15 [MUST]** Re-hook gate: max gap between rehook-class beats (question/reversal/tease/callback) ≤ **120s (20min) / 150s (30min) / 180s (60min)** of VO time; no act >21% of runtime without a reversal. (First-2-minutes tighter cadence per R-3.)
- **R-16 [SHOULD]** Chapters: <25min → no in-player chapters (winners at this length withhold the map); ≥25min → 5–7 curiosity-noun chapters, spoiler blocklist on titles (no guilty/verdict/exonerated/killed/etc.). 45–60min → on-screen part cards, each part a mini-arc with its own hook and cliffhanger.
- **R-17 [MUST]** Fast ending: from final scripted line to end-of-file ≤ **25s (20min) / 60s (30min) / endcard ≤20s (60min)**; BGM terminates on a downbeat (existing ED directive, now a gate). Falling action after the primary reveal only — never new information.
- **R-18 [SHOULD]** Famous-outcome cases run **known-outcome dread** (announce the destination, sell the how — Titan/Man in Cave), not withheld mystery. 45–60min builds require a 30–90s tonal reset after every heavy section in the back half; 30min builds get one 20–40s breather at 55–70%.

### Per-runtime templates (condensed; full text in `pd-structure-template` memory)
- **~20min:** 4 acts; cold open ≤45s; mid reveal 45–55%; payoff 80–100%; re-hook ≤120s; NO chapters; out ≤25s.
- **~30min (main band):** 5 acts (Cooper geometry: Event / Response / Evidence / Deep Dive / Resolution Ladder 65–95%); cold open ≤60s; re-hook ≤150s; 5–7 non-spoiler chapters; outro ≤60s (solved ≤30s); one reset beat 55–70%.
- **45–60min:** 5–6 parts with on-screen cards, 8–11min each, own hook/cliffhanger; re-hook ≤180s inside parts; chapters mandatory; epilogue 2–4min allowed (aftermath only); endcard ≤20s.

## 4. NARRATIVE CRAFT (T4 — Serial / MrBallen / LEMMiNO transcripts verbatim, JCS partial; EP52/EP53 machine-scored)

**Evidence:** winners keep 2–5 loops alive (Serial: 3@25%, 4@50%, 5@75%); three reveal-fuse tiers (10s / 2–5min pronoun-withheld / 10–30min object plants — cigarette butts 18%→89%); villains damned by record inventory, not adjectives; principals humanized by one unrepeatable detail (stuffed reindeer, charm bracelet); registers oscillate (levity resets dread); direct address rationed, **zero emotion commands in ~4 hours of winner material**; specificity 5–12 hard facts/min. Ours: EP52 20/26, EP53 21/26 — failures cluster in emotion-commands (9 and 14 vs ~0), register monotony, no held beats, category-only humanization, EP52's 129s specificity gap.

**Finding:** our loop architecture, honest endings, and record-anchored villainy are already winner-level (house signatures — protect). The gaps are systematic and template-fixable.

**Rules:**
- **R-19 [MUST]** Emotion-command ban: ≤2 narrator imperatives per script, **zero** of the feel/sit-with/think-about class (grep: `Sit with|Think about the|feel the (full )?weight|Now sit inside|Let that sink|aim it at`). Loop-bookkeeping imperatives (Remember X / Keep count) exempt up to 6. Re-stage the fact so the feeling is inevitable instead.
- **R-20 [SHOULD]** Register engineering: ≥3 distinct emotional registers per act; ≥1 relief/warm/dry-wit beat in the first half; one false-relief/hope-spike before a reversal in Acts II–III; one scripted held-beat (isolated slow-read line) before each act's reveal.
- **R-21 [MUST]** Specificity floor: ≥5 hard specifics/min average; no stretch >90s (~270 words) without a number, date, or new proper noun; every major reveal sentence carries a date or number.
- **R-22 [SHOULD]** Humanization & villainy: each principal gets ≥1 unrepeatable concrete detail (categories don't count); ≥1 planted physical object recurs as payoff ≥2min later; villain enters by 25% with one record-anchored damning detail, gets one status/humanizing beat before full damnation; ≥1 verbatim quoted record line per act.
- **R-23 [SHOULD]** Loop bookkeeping: ≥2 loops alive past 25%; ≥1 macro loop (named object/count) spanning ≥50% of runtime; local micro-resolutions every ~3min while macro loops stay open; ≥60s pronoun-withheld delay on one mid-tier reveal.
- **R-24 [MUST]** Voice hygiene: short-punch share 20–35% (≥1 punch ≤5w capping a ≥30w wind-up per act); ≤8 you/your per 1000w, co-investigator nudges only; ≤2 rhetorical questions per 1000w; AI-smell stock-phrase grep stays zero.
- Full 26-check list = `pd-craft-checklist` memory + T4 §3; wire the [M]/[M*] checks into the script self-check pass.

## 10. CONFIDENCE & CAVEATS

- **Retention curves (T1) are official Analytics API, not innertube** — no double-read needed; but only 19/49 videos have curves, all-traffic mixed (no subscriber/new-viewer split until a Studio cookie window). The Studio retention reader's ENGAGEMENT tabId + get_cards body are **unverified** (401 on a dead cookie); first live run must eyeball `_yt_retention_raw/` dumps before trusting parsed output.
- **Innertube-derived CTR numbers** (1.58% channel, per-video table) are single-source Studio innertube reads; the independent scraper previously measured 2.31% on a different window — treat absolute CTR as ±; deltas within the same instrument are trustworthy. Re-measure wave 8/8 should re-pull with a fresh cookie and compare both instruments once.
- **Small-n hazards, explicitly:** AVP rows with 1-digit views (Bearden 3 views); GB/CA/NL/AU AVPs on 22–110 views; demographics from a signed-in sample that may be a few hundred effective views; T2 loser contrast n=4; T1 length-economics bands n=2–15. None of these became MUST rules on their own.
- **Tier-B/knowledge-based content:** T3 rows #8–15 (channel structures) and all T7 opening/reveal notes are knowledge-based, not re-verified (transcript gate + search cap). The three tier-A anchors (JCS/Cooper/Man-in-Cave) carry the quantitative structure claims. T4's JCS column is fragment+secondary; every JCS-derived check is corroborated by ≥1 full transcript exemplar.
- **What the next cookie-window Studio pull must confirm:** ① rel_perf 0–15s ≥0.5 on new-recipe episodes (R-8 verdict); ② the 80–180s cliff flattening on fold-in builds (promotes R-2 to hard gate); ③ EP34/35 curves post-publish; ④ subscriber vs non-subscriber curve split (links the sub-conversion problem to the opening-departure cohort); ⑤ get_cards parser fix from live dumps; ⑥ E1 chapter regression and E3 title A/B read-outs.
- Corpus findings (T7) are about a keyword-harvested sample, not all of YouTube; the innocence-ceiling (~6×) could revise upward off-corpus — but off-corpus giants' packaging (interrogation-drama, not exoneration-relief) supports the directional lesson.
