## ⛔ QUARANTINE — assertions that must never appear

| id | rule |
|---|---|
| ⛔-01 | **Never state or imply that sounding the sirens would have saved lives or changed the outcome.** No source establishes it (AB-01). The permitted form is: the system existed, it was for this, one siren inside the burn perimeter was operable, it had never been used for a wildfire, and it was not used. Then stop |
| ⛔-02 | **Never merge LH-30 and the decision not to activate into one sentence.** They are two separate facts — "only one siren was operable within the burn perimeter" and "the sirens were not sounded" — and collapsing them produces a claim neither source makes. Say each, separately, with its source |
| ⛔-03 | **Never say the sirens "failed."** SRC-0001 says one was operable within the burn perimeter; SRC-0006 says none were activated. "Failed" asserts a third thing that no source says |
| ⛔-04 | **No hint, rhetorical question, or unexplained gap about the cause.** The cause is on the record and the film states it plainly (LH-11, LH-14, LH-16). The film never says the cause is unknown, contested by unnamed people, or "still being asked about." Raising a question and leaving it open is the register of the directed-energy-weapon material this topic sits beside, and this film must be impossible to mistake for it. **Never use the words "some say," "many believe," "questions remain," or "we may never know"** |

## The one-sentence contradiction this film is built on

> Hawaiʻi built the largest outdoor warning siren network in the world and tested it on the first
> business day of every month — and on the day a town burned from the mountain side down, one siren
> inside the burn perimeter was operable, the system had never once been used to warn of a wildfire,
> and the alerts that were sent went to phones on towers that had already gone dark.

Every act must serve that sentence, and ⛔-01 through ⛔-04 govern how it is said.
