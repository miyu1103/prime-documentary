- **ENDING — the count resolved:** back to the wall. Four tallies wash away; one remains. The letter folded, kept. Four caps in cream dawn on the pier. End type: **"THE TRUTH ARRIVED IN AN ENVELOPE."** Restraint returns; the film ends as quietly as it began.
