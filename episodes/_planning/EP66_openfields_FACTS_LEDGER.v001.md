## THE GOVERNING CAUTION FOR THIS WHOLE LEDGER

These are **two courts in two states applying two different state constitutions**, decided **twenty-six
months apart**, and **they did not decide the same thing**:

- Pennsylvania struck two statutes down **on their face** and **overruled its own precedent**.
- Tennessee held the same kind of statute **facially CONSTITUTIONAL** and unconstitutional **only as
  applied** to these two men.

**The Pennsylvania opinion never mentions Rainwaters, Hollingsworth, or the Tennessee Wildlife Resources
Agency** — verified by string search: `Rainwaters` = 0 hits, `Tennessee Wildlife` = 0 hits,
`Hollingsworth` = 0 hits in PA-LEAD. Pennsylvania's Tennessee authority is *Welch v. State*, 289 S.W. 510
(Tenn. 1926), a century-old case. Any line implying one case caused, followed, or cited the other is
fabricated. See ⛔-01.

Neither opinion contains the string `96%`, `Institute for Justice`, or `ij.org`. See ⛔-06.
