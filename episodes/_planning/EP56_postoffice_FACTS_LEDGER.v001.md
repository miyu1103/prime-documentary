## ★ CONFIRMED FACTS (numbered · source · confidence · usage note)

### A. The system (Act I)
- **PO-01 (H)** **Horizon** was built for the Post Office by **ICL Pathway** — ICL was owned by **Fujitsu**; the Inquiry treats ICL Pathway/Fujitsu Services as "Fujitsu." Roll-out began **about September 1999**, complete by **end of 2001/early 2002**, after which **every Post Office branch** ran it. Versions: Legacy Horizon to 2010 → "Horizon Online"/HNG-X from 2010 → HNG-A from 2017, **still in use today**. Src: Vol 1 §§1.1–1.6. Use: plainly. Do NOT give a hard branch count for 1999–2001 (network size then unverified; "more than eleven thousand branches" is safe only for the 2019 network per Fraser No 6).
- **PO-02 (M-H)** Horizon has been described as **"the largest non-military IT contract in Europe"** at the time. Src: Private Eye special report p.1–2 (says "contract", not "project"); phrase widely repeated. Use: attributed — "described at the time as the biggest non-military IT contract in Europe."
- **PO-03 (H)** Named bugs in the court record: the **Callendar Square bug** (Legacy; surfaced 2005 at the Callendar Square, Falkirk branch; duplicated entries; existed "probably" since 2000 per Fujitsu's own witness); the **Receipts and Payments Mismatch bug** (Horizon Online, 2010 — internal Fujitsu/PO note: discrepancies "disappear" at the counter "but will still show within the back end branch account," impacting "circa 40 branches"); the **Dalmellington bug** (Horizon Online, 2015, Ayrshire; duplicated transactions, e.g. a £22,290 discrepancy). The trial "Bug Table" alleged **29 bugs**; the Post Office's OWN expert accepted **11** (Fraser No 6 §930). Src: Fraser No 6 §§404–441, 930. Use: name at most 1–2 bugs in narration; "even the Post Office's own expert accepted eleven" is court-record.
- **PO-04 (H · VERBATIM-SAFE)** Fraser J on the system: **"In summary terms only, Legacy Horizon was not remotely robust."** (Fraser No 6, robustness conclusions); and on the Post Office's posture: **"a simple institutional obstinacy or refusal to consider any possible alternatives to their view of Horizon"** (§928) and **"It amounts to the 21st century equivalent of maintaining that the earth is flat."** (§929 — checked against the judgment text this session, character-exact). Use: §929 = QUOTE_CARD #1; attribute "Mr Justice Fraser, High Court, December 2019."
- **PO-05 (H)** Sub-postmasters were **contractually liable for shortfalls** shown by Horizon and **"There was no facility within the Horizon system for SPMs to dispute Horizon's figures"** (Hamilton §13); the Post Office "relied upon data from Horizon to hold postmasters liable under their contracts for apparent losses… assert[ing] that the losses were real, as opposed to illusory" (Vol 1 §1.8). Src: Hamilton §§12–13; Vol 1 §1.8. Use: the contract trap — plainly.
- **PO-06 (H pattern · ⚠phrasing)** **"You're the only one":** the helpline told caller after caller they were the only one with problems. Inquiry record: Martin Griffiths "was made to feel that he was the only person who was having balancing problems, **a complaint made repeatedly by a large number of postmasters who contacted the Helpdesk**" (Vol 1 §3.47); Maureen McKelvey "was told by Post Office employees that she was the only person who was experiencing problems" (Vol 1 §3.174). Src: Vol 1 (verbatim-checked). Use: present as the documented pattern — "sub-postmaster after sub-postmaster told the inquiry the helpline said they were the only one."

### C. The prosecutor (Act II) & what they knew (Act III)
- **PO-17 (H)** **Scale of prosecution** — use the Inquiry's own finding first: **"it seems to me to be likely that approximately 1,000 persons were prosecuted and convicted throughout the United Kingdom… based on Horizon evidence"** (Vol 1 §3.24, verbatim-extracted), plus **50–60 prosecuted but acquitted** (§3.25). - **PO-18 (H)** The Post Office acted as **victim, investigator AND prosecutor in the same cases** — bringing its own private prosecutions with **no police investigation and no CPS charging decision**. IMPORTANT NUANCE: this was the ordinary right of any person or company in England & Wales to bring a private prosecution, NOT a special statutory power — never say "unique powers"; the horror is the industrial scale + the triple role. Court of Appeal: **"From about 2015, POL ceased to conduct its own prosecutions, and the Crown Prosecution Service became the prosecuting authority"** (Hamilton §28 — verbatim-checked). Prosecutions on Horizon data ran ~1999/2000 to autumn 2013 (Vol 1 §1.7). Private Eye: prosecutions fell **from 42 in 2012/13 to two in 2013/14 and zero the next year** — the collapse coinciding with Second Sight's arrival. - **PO-28 (H · VERBATIM-SAFE)** **Hamilton & others, Court of Appeal (Holroyde LJ, Picken J, Farbey J), 23 April 2021**: **39 of 42** convictions quashed.
