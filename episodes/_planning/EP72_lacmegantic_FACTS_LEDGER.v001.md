## 1. THE TRAIN AND THE NIGHT

| id | fact | grade | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| LM-01 | Train **MMA-002** of the Montreal, Maine & Atlantic Railway carried **72 Class 111 tank cars** of petroleum crude oil loaded at New Town, North Dakota | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-02 | The cargo was **7.7 million litres** of crude | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-03 | At about **22:50 on 5 July 2013** the train stopped at **Nantes, Quebec**, the designated MMA crew change point | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 |
| LM-04 | It was parked **on a descending grade on the main track**, **just over seven miles** from Lac-Mégantic | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-05 | The engineer secured the train and went to a hotel in the town | SECONDARY | SRC-0009 |
| LM-06 | At about **23:50** a local resident reported **a fire on the lead locomotive, MMA 5017**, to the 911 emergency call centre | VERBATIM | SRC-0001 |
| LM-07 | Firefighters **shut off the locomotive's fuel supply** and **moved the electrical breakers inside the cab to the off position** | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-08 | With all the locomotives shut down, **the air compressor no longer supplied air to the air brake system** | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-09 | Hand brakes had been applied on **five locomotives and two other cars** | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-10 | The investigation concluded that **a minimum of 17 and possibly as many as 26 hand brakes** were actually needed | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-11 | The brake effectiveness test was conducted **with the locomotive air brakes still applied**, which prevented it from revealing that the hand brakes alone were insufficient | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-12 | Just before 01:00 the air pressure fell below the point at which the combination of air and hand brakes could hold the train, and it began to roll | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-13 | It derailed near the centre of the town at **about 01:15**, having reached a **top speed of 65 mph** | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-14 | **63 of the 72 tank cars derailed** | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-15 | Approximately **six million litres** were released | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-16 | **47 people died** | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-17 | Much of the downtown core was destroyed | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-18 | 30 of the 47 died at the Musi-Café | SECONDARY | SRC-0009 |
| LM-19 | More than thirty buildings in the town centre were destroyed, and all but three of the remaining thirty-nine had to be demolished because of petroleum contamination | SECONDARY | SRC-0009 |

## 2. THE EIGHT MONTHS BEFORE

| id | fact | grade | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| LM-20 | In **October 2012**, eight months before the accident, the lead locomotive was sent to MMA's repair shop after an engine failure | VERBATIM | SRC-0004 |
| LM-21 | Given the time and cost of a standard repair and the pressure to return the locomotive to service, the engine was repaired with **an epoxy-like polymeric material that lacked the required strength and durability** | VERBATIM | SRC-0004 |
| LM-22 | The cam bearing fractured when its mounting bolt was over-tightened after being installed as part of that non-standard repair | VERBATIM | SRC-0004 |
| LM-23 | Failure of the cam bearing reduced oil supply to the valve train; the loss of lubrication led to valve damage and a punctured piston crown; oil accumulated in the turbocharger, overheated, and caught fire **on the night of the derailment** | VERBATIM | SRC-0004 |

## 3. WHAT THE INVESTIGATION FOUND

| id | fact | grade | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| LM-24 | The TSB identified **18 distinct causes and contributing factors** | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-25 | The investigation **was not able to conclude that having another crew member would have prevented the accident** | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-26 | MMA **developed a safety management system in 2002**, did not begin implementing it **until 2010**, and by 2013 it was **still not functioning effectively** | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-27 | Transport Canada's Quebec office **did not audit that system until 2010** | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-28 | The crude oil **was more volatile than described on the shipping documents** | VERBATIM | SRC-0002 |
| LM-29 | The TSB called for additional physical defences against runaway trains and more thorough audits of safety management systems | VERBATIM | SRC-0003 |
| LM-30 | Transport Canada does not validate a railway company's special instructions and gives no specific guidance on how many hand brakes must be applied to a parked freight train | SECONDARY | SRC-0009 |

## 5. WHAT THE RECORD DOES NOT HAVE — and the film says so

| id | absence |
|---|---|
| AB-01 | **No individual is established as the cause.** Eighteen factors were found; a jury acquitted the three men charged. The film's ending is this absence, not a culprit |
| AB-02 | Whether a second crew member would have changed the night is **not established** (LM-25) |
| AB-03 | The single-person operating authority is **context, not a finding**: it is not among the 18 causes and contributing factors |
| AB-04 | We have **not** read the trial transcript, the Quebec Superior Court file, or the full 200-page TSB report end to end. Every row above is either from the TSB's own summary and lab report or is marked SECONDARY |

## ⛔ QUARANTINE — assertions that must never appear

| id | rule |
|---|---|
| ⛔-01 | Never state or imply that **Harding, Labrie or Demaître** was at fault, negligent or responsible. Wherever any of them is described, the **acquittal appears in the same breath**. This binds the title, the thumbnail text, the description and every figure card |
| ⛔-02 | Never assert that **a second crew member would have prevented it** (LM-25) |
| ⛔-03 | Never assert that **single-person operation caused** the derailment. It is not among the 18 factors |
| ⛔-04 | Never give a **single number** for the hand brakes required. The form is always "seven were applied; the investigation concluded a minimum of seventeen were needed, and possibly as many as twenty-six" |
| ⛔-05 | **No victim is named, shown or characterised.** 47 people died and the film names none of them. No Musi-Café interior reconstruction with people in it |
| ⛔-06 | Never assert the crude was **deliberately** mislabelled. The record establishes the documents described it as less volatile than it was; intent is not established |
| ⛔-07 | The claim that Transport Canada imposed **69 conditions** on another railway's single-person operations against **one** for MMA appears in secondary and advocacy sources only. It is **not in this ledger as a fact** and may not be narrated until read in a primary source |
| ⛔-08 | Any Ed Burkhardt quotation must be verified in a primary source before use, or attributed explicitly as reported by a named outlet |
| ⛔-09 | No figure for deaths, litres, speed, grade, distance or money that is not a row above |
| ⛔-10 | Never depict a burning town with people in frame. The fire is shown as light, smoke, and its aftermath at a distance — never as a casualty |
