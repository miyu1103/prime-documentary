  - **§ 804(j)(3) the override:** *"Any applicable statute of repose or statute of limitations, other than under paragraph (2), shall not apply to a claim under this section."*
  - **§ 804(j)(2)** set the filing window at two years — **it closed 10 August 2024** for practically everyone. ⚠ ★**R3 NUANCE:** the subsection is a **"later of"** clause — (A) two years after enactment **or** (B) *"180 days after the date on which the claim is denied under section 2675"* — so a claimant whose administrative claim was denied late retains 180 days from that denial. **Never say the door slammed shut on 10 August 2024 without a hedge;** the script says *"for almost everyone it closed on the tenth of August 2024."* § 804(h) required an administrative claim to the Navy first (via 28 U.S.C. § 2675), with a **six-month** wait. **H.**
  - ★**R3 note on jury (CL-38):** the strike order is **still controlling as of 29 July 2026.** The Fourth Circuit denied a mandamus petition seeking to overturn it (*In re McBrine*, No. 24-1542, 23 Aug 2024 — **M**, from a search index, not the order itself), the district court declined interlocutory certification, and a 2025 bill to restore jury rights by statute has not become law. **No appellate court has ruled on the merits.**
