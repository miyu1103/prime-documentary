## 2. The five finalists — scored against the binding gate

Scored with `py -3.11 scripts/score_premise.py --score <premise>.json` against
`config/pd_planning_os.v002.json`. Premise files: `runs/premises/EP72-73/`.

| 2 | **Merrimack Valley gas explosions** | 110/120 | 92 | ×3 | **107** | PRIORITY |
| 3 | **Lac-Mégantic** | 105/120 | 88 | ×3 | **103** | PRIORITY |
| 4 | **Massachusetts drug lab** | 110/120 | 92 | ×2 | **102** | PRIORITY |
| 5 | **El Faro** | 103/120 | 86 | ×3 | **101** | PRIORITY |

Why these five and not the other 45, in one line each:

- **GM ignition switch** — the only candidate that scores full marks on personal relevance, title power and broad audience at once. The contradiction is a *document* — a part number that did not change — which is exactly what this channel can put on screen.
- **Merrimack Valley** — the same gas regulator is in the viewer's own street, the image is a residential road burning at both ends, and the case is closed with a criminal conviction.
- **Lac-Mégantic** — the strongest single thumbnail available on the whole slate, and the contradiction is regulatory rather than personal, which keeps it out of victim-blaming.
- **Massachusetts drug lab** — the largest mass dismissal of convictions in US history, and the only finalist whose reversal is on the scale of tens of thousands of people.
- **El Faro** — a 26-hour bridge recording is a structural gift for a 30-minute film: the film can be the tape, and the audience knows the ending from the first minute.

### Honest risks on the finalists

1. **GM is adjacent to EP68 pinto**, which publishes 2026-08-22. Both are "a car company weighed a known defect". The mechanisms differ (a cost memo vs an unchanged part number) but running them within a month reads as repetition. If EP72 is GM, EP73 should not be from lane B.
2. **Every finalist still needs the demand ÷ incumbent probe.** `TOPIC_PIPELINE.v005` §1 rejected two topics that looked strong on the rubric alone, and this rubric cannot see incumbents. Five probes cost 500 units.
3. **Lac-Mégantic and El Faro are non-US settings**; the channel's own measurement shows GB/AU retain 2.4–2.8× the US, so this is a smaller risk than it looks, but it is not zero.
