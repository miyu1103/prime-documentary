## 0. What this episode's thumbnail has to do, and why it is hard

The hero image of this film is **two bills that differ by one letter in a name** — `Willie C. Craft`
against `Willie S. Craft` (ML-19). That is the whole story in one picture, and it is the one picture
this episode **may not make**:

> `forbidden_subjects`: *"readable text, numerals, signage, logos, seals or emblems in any generated image."*
> Codex order §1: *"読める文字・数字・署名・印章・ロゴを描かない。特に請求書・最終通知・メーターの目盛り・
> 台帳カードは繰り返し画面に出ます。すべて判読不能に描く。ここが事故の最頻発点です。"*

**Saying it twice, because it is the failure this thumbnail set exists to avoid:
the difference between the two documents must never be shown as readable type.**
Not a rendered letter, not a blurred letter that resolves at 1280 px, not a highlighted character.

**The compositional solution, and it is the only one permitted here.**
Show **two documents that are identical in every visible respect**, then make the frame say
*one of them is not the same* **with light and colour, not with letters**:

| Device | How it reads | Where it is applied |
|---|---|---|
| **Two objects, same size, same fold, same angle, overlapping** | "these are a pair" | in the generated plate |
| **One hard circle of light** falling on the address window of one of them | "the difference lives here" | in the generated plate (`M219`) or as a composited gold focal ring (`M208`/`M209`) |
| **Everything outside that circle dissolved toward grey** (desaturated, one stop down, detail retained) | "nothing else about them differs" | composite step, `ImageEnhance.Color` on a radial mask |
| **A single gold `#E5B53A` ring or bracket** around the lit detail | the viewer's eye lands there in <0.3 s at 320 px | composite step |

The lit window stays **empty of glyphs**. The viewer is told *where* the difference is and never shown
*what* it is — which is also, exactly, what the film is about.

## 2. The look

### 2.1 Ground
Near-black to deep navy (`#05070C` → `#0C1420`), lit from **one side only**. Nothing centred and evenly
lit — the Codex order rejects catalogue lighting (`§0.5`: 距離・光・瞬間).

### 2.2 Accents (pick ONE accent per variant, never both)
- **Gold `#E5B53A`** — the focal ring, the kicker rule, the second headline line.
- **Electric blue `#1F6BFF`** — reserved for `THUMB-03` (the telephone) so the four options are not
  four versions of the same picture.
- **White / silver `#F5F5F5`** — the first headline line, always with a **black stroke ≥ 8 px**
  (`build_case_thumbnails_v002.stroked`, `sw=8`) so it survives any background.

### 2.3 Type
- Font `remotion/public/fonts/ariblk.ttf` (headline), `arialbd.ttf` (kicker).
- Headline **two lines max**, **3–4 words total**, cap height **≥ 78 px**.
- Kicker: a filled tag, **≤ 3 words**, gold or red `#D22628`, upper-left or lower-left.
- Bottom scrim: black, 0 → 72 % alpha over the lower 38 % of the frame.

### 2.4 The luma budget — the one that fails silently

`thumbnail_visibility` is a **HARD** gate and it measures **the selected file only**:
`mean luma ≥ 33.0`, `contrast std ≥ 40.0` (calibrated 2026-07-04: rejected dark v001 = 24.9–29.5;
approved bright v002 = 36.4–48.0).

This episode's whole visual register is *unlit rooms, low-key, muted, flat humid Tennessee light*.
**A faithful frame from this film will fail the gate.** So the thumbnail is built, not cropped:

1. `ImageEnhance.Brightness(hero, 1.25–1.45)` then `ImageEnhance.Contrast(·, 1.20)` then
   `ImageEnhance.Color(·, 1.15)` — the same ladder `build_case_thumbnails_v002.build_hero` uses.
2. A **focal glow** (radial white at 12–18 % alpha) on the subject, `focus` given per variant below.
3. The **white headline block itself contributes** — roughly 6–9 luma points across the frame at this
   cap height. Do not shrink the type to "balance" the composition; it is load-bearing for R10.
4. **Measure before selecting.** Run the probe on every candidate, not just the favourite:

```powershell
py -3.11 -c "from PIL import Image, ImageStat; import sys, pathlib; [print(p.name, ['%.1f' % v for v in (lambda s: (s.mean[0], s.stddev[0]))(ImageStat.Stat(Image.open(p).convert('L')))]) for p in sorted(pathlib.Path(r'episodes/PD-2026-064-memphis/10_thumbnail').glob('*.png'))]"
```

Target **mean ≥ 38, std ≥ 45** so the gate has margin; 33/40 is the floor, not the aim.

### 6.1 Title locks — a title that breaks one of these is not shipped

| # | Never | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **T1** | **"They were overcharged" / "the utility billed them twice" / "she didn't owe it"** — in any form, including "wrongly", "unfairly", "for nothing" | ⛔-02, ML-86, ML-119. **Nobody ever decided whether the Crafts owed the money.** The trial court found only *possible* gas overcharges (ML-52); the dissent found the two accounts genuinely independent (ML-99). The ledger names this "the single most likely factual failure in the episode." |
| **T2** | **"Now they must hold a hearing first" / "the Court stopped the shutoffs" / "they can't cut you off any more"** | ⛔-04, ⛔-06, ML-76. The Court **kept the utility's power to cut service** after affording the opportunity. A hearing before a shutoff is not the end of shutoffs. |
| **T3** | **Any present-tense claim about what a utility must do** — "your utility has to…", "today", "still", "modern rules" | ⛔-07, ○-04. Nothing in this 1978 opinion establishes a current rule, and the property interest rests entirely on **Tennessee law binding a municipal utility**. |
| **T4** | **Any national number** — households cut off per year, disputes nationwide, the 16 % figure | ⛔-09, ○-03, ○-08. The only shutoff numbers in the document are Memphis's own (≈2,000/month, 11,216 in six months, 33,000 high-bill complaints in 1973). 16 % is a New York figure from a different case in a footnote. |
| **T5** | **A vote count** — "6–3", "the Court split", naming who joined Powell | ⛔-01, ML-04. The opinion states no vote and no join list. Powell wrote; three Justices dissented. |
| **T6** | **"Class action" / "it changed the rules for every Memphis customer"** | ⛔-12, ML-47/53/92. Certification was refused and the refusal was affirmed. This is a judgment about two people at one address. |
| **T7** | **An ending for the family** — "they won", "they got their money back", "they finally…" | ⛔-05, ○-01, ML-86. Damages went back down undecided and the record stops there. |
| **T8** | **A season, a date, a duration or a sum for the five terminations** — especially **"winter"** | ⛔-03, ML-24. The opinion supplies none of it. The contract's own THREAT line says 冬に; that is a packaging hypothesis, **not a licence for the title**. |

**Note on "five".** ML-118 permits *five* **attributed to the Court** (the dissent says "several"). A
60-character title cannot carry the attribution cleanly, so **no shipped title above uses the number.**
If a later variant wants it, it must read "the Court said five times", not "five times".
