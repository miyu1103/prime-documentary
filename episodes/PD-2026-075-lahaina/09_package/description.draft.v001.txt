On 8 August 2023 a fire burned through Lahaina, on Maui. The County of Maui's origin and cause report, published on 3 October 2024, records that it claimed at least 102 lives.

The cause is on the record and it is not disputed here. The County's fire department, working with the federal Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives, found that the fire originated off Lahainaluna Road at 6:34 in the morning, in unmaintained vegetation near utility pole 25, from molten metallic material ejected by the re-energization of broken power lines above. The classification is Accidental.

This film is about the warning. Hawaii operates the largest single integrated outdoor warning siren system in the world, tests it on the first business day of every month, and lists wildfires among the hazards it is for. The state's own independent investigation, carried out by the Fire Safety Research Institute for the Attorney General of Hawaii, produced 518 pages, 84 findings and 140 recommendations. Finding 37 records that only one siren was operable within the burn perimeter of the Lahaina area on 8 August 2023. Finding 38 records that the system had not been used to warn of a wildland-urban interface fire before that day.

And not one of those 84 findings says that sounding it would have changed the outcome. This film does not say so either. It reports what the record holds and stops where the record stops.

What it also holds: the first evacuation order in the county's own public alert log that names a Lahaina neighbourhood is timestamped 16:16 — sent to cellphones in a region where, in the investigation's words, high winds had destroyed cellular phone communication that same morning. Both water systems had uninterrupted power and produced at capacity throughout, and the pressure fell anyway, because every burning house became an open tap. Two of the eight roads out of Front Street were gone by seven that evening. And the emergency operations centre sign-in sheet for that day was never produced.

Every factual line in the narration is sourced to one of those. Where the record does not carry something — the acreage, the wind speed actually measured on that slope, whether the siren would have changed anything — the film says so out loud instead of filling the gap.

ON THE SETTLEMENT, AS OF AUGUST 2026
A global settlement of 4.037 billion dollars was announced in August 2024. Award determination notices went out on 17 June 2026. As of August 2026 no payment has been made: an appeal over how much of the settlement is paid in legal fees is pending before the Hawaii Supreme Court, and while it is pending no settlement payment can be made.

ABOUT THE IMAGES
This film uses AI-assisted visualizations. They are symbolic reconstructions, not photographs of the event, they are labelled on screen where they depict the day itself, and they contain no likeness of any real person. No victim is named, shown or described anywhere in this film.
