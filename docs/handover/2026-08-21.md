## 2. i2v invents people, and nothing on the ship path was looking

**Measured on all 160 EP70 clips, 6 frames each, 15 contact sheets: 32 rejected (20%).** Every
one puts a person in a frame whose plate has none. The worst are not near-misses — W044 walks a
woman up to the front door of a house that is not hers, in a film about an FBI team raiding the
wrong family's home; W027 resolves a face; W020 puts a woman over the splintered lock, the film's
central image. W122 is rejected for a different reason: the house number 4112 degrades to 411Z
across the animation, and the case is *about* the address.

**The rejection is not uniform and that is the useful part.** Plates that imply human presence —
houses, doors, streets, domestic interiors (W001-W075) — failed at **36%**. Plates of documents,
courthouse interiors and 1970s offices (W076-W160) failed at **7%**. The subject decides, not the
seed. EP72 lacmegantic's bible already bars people from its fire imagery, so this is checkable in
advance for the next episodes.

**The two documented remedies do not work.** `i2v_episode_batch.py` carries a note from EP61
weimer about exactly this and offers `--prompt`, `--neg` and `--seed-base`. W012 and W035 were
regenerated with a negative listing person/people/human/figure/face and a fresh seed: both came
back with the same figures. `comfy_wan.py` wires the negative into the graph correctly, so it was
applied and Wan overrode it. **The defect has to be caught, not prevented.**

`scripts/qc_motion_clips.py` (new) builds the sheets. **Six frames is the floor** — a 3-frame
sheet was built first and passed W044, whose woman appears only mid-clip.

**Hands and gloved arms are KEPT.** They are this episode's own register — agents handling
documents — and a hand is not an identifiable likeness.

## 3. Quarantining by hand does not hold. Use the blocklist.

The 32 rejects were moved out of `motion/` and came back **twice**: `_finish_episode.sh` [1/7]
rebuilds mp4s from the frame dirs and [2/7] re-copies from the source archive. The second time
they were already in the pool when the render started.

The mechanism for this already existed and says so in its own comment: *"the source archive is
intentionally immutable, so a rejected i2v can reappear every time the render-visible pool is
refreshed."* They are now rows in `config/footage_blocklist.v001.json`, scoped
`"episodes": ["wronghouse"]`, class `cat2_real_person_likeness`, and [2b/7] prunes them on every
run: `img: 0 removed / 160 remain, motion: 32 removed / 128 remain`.

Note the matcher is stem-based, so a `.mp4` row also matches the `.png` plate. That is why the
row is episode-scoped and why the plates must be checked after a prune.
