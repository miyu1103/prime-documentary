(`H077_depth.png`) as cut 77. Cause: `build_asset_manifest_motionfirst.py` and
`i2v_episode_batch.py` both glob `H*` and swept the `_depth` companions in, so 62 of the 71

demonstrated failing (180 cuts) on the defective film. The depth artefacts are quarantined in
`remotion/public/lahaina/_motion_depth_rejected_20260825/`, `.../img_depth/` and
`ae-demo/_rejected_depth_frames_lahaina/`. A real i2v of 91 hero plates then ran to completion,
and the rebuilt film is **338 cuts, 98.5 % video, 0 depth**.

**EP74 itaewon's 00:12 re-render still carried the wrong-country class at scale.** The five named

## 2. Three instruments that lied, all fixed and demonstrated

* **`check_plate_verdicts` called i2v-converted plates "REGENERATED".** A plate that leaves `img/`
  is resolved to its `motion/<id>.mp4` and that file was hashed against a verdict bound to the
  PNG — shas that can never match. EP76 morandi reported all 60 converted plates as regenerated
  and the build was sent to re-review 60 pictures nobody had touched. It now hashes the still
  where the finisher retires it. Both directions demonstrated: morandi goes PASS/READY, and a
  deliberately poisoned sha in a copy of the verdict file is still caught.
* **The finisher retired the people plates the spec declares.** `retire_unused_pool_clips.py`
  moves stills the current film does not reference into `img_unused/`, including declared
  `people_plates`; `check_episode_inputs` then counted what was left and refused the NEXT build
  for having too few people. Every finisher run left the episode unbuildable — itaewon lost 5
  plates, lahaina 4, and both were restored by hand more than once before the cause was found.
  Declared `people_plates` and `mandatory_stills` are now protected (measured: itaewon retires 0,
  was 29).
* **A face detector called a corridor, a hydrant and two siren poles "faces".**
  `register_face_stills` registered 4 such plates for lahaina. Reading all 91 hero plates at full
  size settled the likeness question instead: **no lahaina plate shows a face at all.** The
  face-on shots the shipped-frames review reported at 1:58, 5:42, 11:27, 19:28, 23:29, 29:12 and
  29:36 were grayscale depth maps misread as faces — H007/H019/H041/H043 hold a town, a siren
  pole, a house and a broken wire. Recorded in `episode_spec.v003`.

## 3. Long jobs must start with `run_in_background`, not `pd_run.sh`

The itaewon render died twice at ~12,000 frames with `rc=127` and **not one error line in the
log** — 96 GB RAM free, GPU idle, no crash event. Both deaths landed inside a tool call of this
session. Last night's identical render, launched directly, ran three hours. The nohup'd child of
`pd_run.sh` does not survive; a harness-tracked background task does. Everything since has been
launched that way and has stayed alive. `pd_run.sh`'s cheap-proof-first discipline is still
right — run the smoke check by hand, take the lock, then launch the job as a tracked background
task.
