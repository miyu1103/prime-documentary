**Only four classes may stop a ship:** an identifiable real person or minor in frame; uncleared rights or
licence; a factual claim without support or contradicted by the record; a fabricated record presented as
authentic. **Everything else is advisory** — measured, recorded to
`episodes/<EPID>/09_package/release_deviations.v001.json` with its number, and shipped.

Nothing was weakened. Every check still runs and every number is still written down. What changed is
which numbers hold the door shut, and that decision now lives in the scheduler rather than in judgement.

Three corollaries, all binding:
- **Never rebuild a finished master for a defect measured in seconds.** Ladder: ship-and-record → fix in
  the next revision → splice the affected range (~10-15 min) → full re-render (1h35m-2h10m) only when a
  *blocking* defect is distributed across the film.
- **Book the slot, then improve against the date.** A private video with a future publishAt is reversible
  until it publishes. A day not shipped is gone.
- **Advisory findings are decided by whoever is doing the work.** On 08-11 roughly fifteen decisions went
  to the owner in one night; that is its own form of blocking.

## 2. The five instruments that lied

This is the pattern of the night, and it is worth more than any individual fix. In almost every case the
film was fine and the *measurement* was wrong.

1. **`caption_sync` on correa** — measured the pre-polish srt, which the render never burns. Real value

2. **`arc_nonrepeat`** — compared *filenames*. `marmet/img/R015.png` and `ramirez/img/R015.png` both

   **openfields was being waved through with 10 genuine reuses** — the same footage re-ingested under
   different ids, which name matching structurally cannot see.
3. **The footage matcher** — `all(t in title.lower() for t in terms)`, a substring test with no word
   boundaries. `old car` matched "h**old**ing pla**car**ds" and pulled **2020 BLM protest footage** into
   a 1970s film; `arch`→marching, `trial`→industrial, `gas`→las vegas, `corn`→popcorn. **100 of 807
   candidates (12.4%)** matched inside another word. Fixed, word-boundary aware, 1.78× faster.
4. **`forbidden_subjects` was never applied at harvest.** The config file *claimed* the 124 terms were
   filtered; no code did it. **19 of pinto's 465 queries were themselves forbidden subjects.** Now
   enforced, and a query contradicting the spec is a hard error at config load.
5. **The spec revision was invisible.** `check_episode_spec.spec_path()` was hardcoded to
   `episode_spec.v001.json` — *"One name, no revision search."* A correctly written v002 would have been
   read by nothing. Every spec correction made tonight would have been cosmetic.

**Before believing a red, re-measure it.** A large share of the five lost days was this.
