## 5. The instruments that lied, in order

This is the pattern of the three days and is worth more than any single fix.

1. **`publishAt` is not a schedule.** EP64 carried a date, a thumbnail and captions while
   `uploadStatus` was still `uploaded` and `processingStatus` still `processing`, eleven hours
   after a force-kill interrupted its 1.7 GB transfer. It would have reached its slot broken.
   `pd_watchdog.py` now asks the live API every 15 minutes and alerts on any scheduled video that
   is not `processed/succeeded`.
2. **A counter that measures an intermediate breaks when you clean the intermediate.** Reclaiming
   24.5 GB of spent Wan frame directories made every progress counter read 157 finished
   conversions as zero. Caught before the chain acted on it; all three now count the mp4s.
3. **A people-plate converted to motion is still a people plate.** `check_episode_inputs` counted
   only `img/`, so ramirez was refused for "too few people" at the exact moment 16 of its 24
   people plates became motion clips.
4. **A probe that matches itself.** The process check contained the string it searched for and
   reported 1 process on an idle machine. Excluded its own signature; verified the zero case.
5. **cp932.** `subprocess.run(text=True)` decodes with the locale codec. One non-ASCII byte from a
   child killed greene's upload *between* "uploaded" and "publishAt set" — the whole reason 08-16
   was lost.
6. **A step with nothing to do killed four builds.** `assemble_episode_i2v` ran `mkdir` on H: as
   its first statement, so a detached drive failed step [1/7] for four episodes that had zero
   frames to assemble.
7. **`shutil.copy` onto itself.** With the archive gone, out_dir *is* pub_dir; `SameFileError`
   killed the assembler on the first clip of every chunk, so openfields finished 53 conversions
   and only 10 mp4s reached the pool. The i2v looked complete.
8. **One error message, four different diseases.** `_finish_episode.sh` reports any non-zero exit
   from the audio dry-run as "the 4-layer mix would fail its density gate". Actually: openfields
   was *passing* and died later at the mux; ramirez and hyatt had no `03_script` in `[VO:]` form;
   only pinto had a real density problem.
9. **A quarantine rule ignored its own condition.** `check_script_craft` substring-matched every
   quoted string in a ⛔ row, so EP67 was blocked by the attributed Thomas quote the ledger
   *requires*, by both courts' phrases quoted side by side (the approved anti-merge form), and by
   the words "Article III" in a film about Article III. Rules now carry a mode read from the row's
   own text; six adversarial demonstrations pass.
10. **A stale review bound to fresh frames.** correa's FAIL came from two `real_person_likeness`
    rejections describing a woman and toddler and a masked man. The frames at those keys are an
    empty waiting room and an empty corridor; sheets 21 and 77 contain no such person; the named
    assets are in neither film json. All 78 sheets were re-read: no identifiable person anywhere.
    See `runs/qc/correa_review_v001_not_corroborated.v001.md`.
11. **hyatt's title was a real overreach the gate caught.** "A Fabricator Asked to Use Two Rods"
    states as fact what CH-07 says nobody has ever established. Retitled to what the record holds.
    This one was the gate being *right* — the other ten were it being wrong.
