## 1. Staged ≠ sourced. Four threads hit this independently.

`stage_footage_by_title.py` was the right discovery — it reads the ledger's human-written
**titles** instead of the pervasively-wrong filenames, and it staged 335 clips for EP70 in about
ten minutes after four failed attempts with filename search. That part holds.

**What does not hold is treating the staged count as a supply number.** 335 was filtered to 177
by machine rules, `solve_totals(2398.7, 177, ...)` was run on that 177, and the resulting
"5.73 s per cut, better than shipped hyatt" was reported to the owner as a plan. **No pixel was
examined at any point in that chain.**

Today `check_pool_frames.py --slug wronghouse` built 115 sheets. Sheets 1, 40 and 95 — sampled
at even spacing across the pool, not cherry-picked — were read:

| sheet | contents | usable in a 2017 Georgia wrong-house FBI raid film |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Mongolian-looking green mountain village with a train; pink bokeh with black pillarbox bars; night traffic bokeh | 0 |
| 40 | ivy-covered European ruins; a **CG** glowing church portal | 0 |
| 95 | golf-course aerial; white modernist concert hall; magnolia blossom | 0 |

Three sheets, zero clips. The other threads measured the same rate independently and were all
more careful than the main thread was:

- EP74 itaewon: 18 clips read at 6 frames → **4 accepted, 14 rejected (22%)**. That thread
  explicitly refused to let its "326 staged" number be used in a solve, and was right.
- EP75 lahaina: 50 of 135 read → **40 rejected**.
- EP76 morandi: 61 "already confirmed" clips re-read at full size → **32 fell**, most of them
  ones that thread had itself accepted from small tiles.
- EP72/73: measured its own acceptance rate at **about 30%** and named the shape exactly —
  what comes back is *generic world stock* (Tokyo, Brooklyn Bridge, a Paris street sweeper, a
  hamster, a roulette wheel; Seoul, Kyoto, Chernobyl, a snowed-in temple).

**So the rule is: the only footage number that may enter `solve_totals` is the post-review one.**
A staged count is a search result, not an asset.

The one procedure that measurably works, from EP75: **read the first contact sheet, then write
the second query from what you saw.** That thread's sheets 27 onward (second-round queries) were
almost all hits, against a heavy rejection rate in the first round.

Six frames is the floor for a motion sheet (EP70 W044 passed a 3-frame sheet). For pool frames
the tool already samples 6–12.

## 3. EP76's sound is not done — 42 of its 64 cues resolve to silence

The EP76 thread reported "SFX 64 / 2.07 per min" as complete. Measured by importing
`build_case_film_audio.map_oneshot()` and `HARD_WORDS` and passing every cue through them:

```
cues written into the script .............. 64
picked up (i.e. following a [VO:] line) ... 64   <- placement is fine
resolve to a sample ....................... 22
fall silent ............................... 42
```

Silent kinds: `stone` 11, `metal` 8, `wind` 7, `pen` 6, `room` 6, `wire` 2, `water` 1, `chair` 1.
Real density is **22 / ~30.9 min ≈ 0.7 per min** against a 2.0 floor — [4d] will stop it.

That thread also used a non-canonical cue syntax, `(SFX: stone -- on 'concrete')` rather than
`(SFX: stone "concrete")`. The parser at `build_case_film_audio.py:456` takes the whole body and
substring-matches, so the syntax survives; **the kinds are what fail.**

This is the exact failure the EP75 thread's `--dry-run`/unmapped check was built to prevent, and
the EP75 thread verified its own plan by running the mixer standalone and reading `unmapped=0`
rather than counting its own placements. That is the procedure.

## 5. EP70's motion/still rebalance (done, but built on the bad pool)

Three mechanical attempts to rank the 144 motion clips all failed to discriminate: a
tblend/signalstats energy score returned 0.00 for every clip (broken filter chain),
`freezedetect` called 143 of 144 non-frozen, and source duration is **3.37 s for all 144**.

So the split was made structurally. **Not first-half/second-half** — that would have left the
back half a slideshow, which is the complaint this rebalance exists to answer — but **evenly
spread**: clip *i* is kept iff `floor((i+1)*80/144) > floor(i*80/144)`. The 64 demoted mp4s were
moved to `E:/pd-media/assets/ai_video/wronghouse/motion_demoted_to_still/`, which is both
reversible and out of reach of `[2/7]`'s `cp -n` (hand-quarantine inside `motion/` came back
twice on 08-21).

Result: `stills=75 people=26 motion=80 factory=177`, solving to **438 cuts / 5.48 s**.
**Then the pool review invalidated the 177, so this must be redone after the sheets are read.**
