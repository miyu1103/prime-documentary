## The one finding that matters beyond this episode

The narration master was generated and **came in 54 seconds over the ceiling**: measured 1,964.8 s
and **184.3 wpm**, against a projection of 198.4 wpm from `--measure-section ACT_1`.

Per-section rates, measured from `narration_index.v001.json`:

| HOOK | OP | ACT_1 | ACT_2 | ACT_3 | ACT_4 | ACT_5 | ENDING |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 212.4 | 205.0 | **195.5** | 194.0 | 182.2 | 181.9 | **174.7** | 187.4 |

**`--measure-section ACT_1` is the right instrument pointed at the wrong act.** ACT_1 is the
third-*fastest* section in the film and runs **6.1 % ahead of the whole**. A film whose register
slows as it argues will always be over-estimated by an early-act measurement.

> **Rule for the next episode: measure ACT_1 AND the longest late act, and size from the mean.**
> One act is better than the 171.79 model. It is still biased.

The correction was **220 words**, most of them from ACT_5 at 0.344 s per word — the most expensive
prose in the film, and the act `SCENE_PLAN.v002` §2.5 had already flagged as a structural debt.
**No sourced fact was cut.** Every removal is a second rendering of something the film has already
said, or a signpost. Script **v007: 5,464 words, projected 31:41, 18 s of headroom.**

## The footage lane — the counts were lying, and the frames said so

`alley` measures 10 rows and is the film's central object. Opening the candidate frames:

- **`alley` returns the BOTANICAL sense** — avenues of trees in parks and boulevards
  (`AR-v_123 "alley trees strolling woman walking"`, `AR-v_142 "boulevard park alley trees"`), plus a
  **green-screen** medieval brick corridor and a **cartoon** street. **REMOVED.**
- **`train station`** — chosen precisely to dodge the International-Space-Station trap in bare
  `station` — returns **Eastern European passenger platforms with a smiling, fully identifiable woman
  across six frames**. Wrong country, wrong register, real-person-likeness hazard. **REMOVED.**
- **`seoul` measures only 15 rows and one of them is a real Seoul subway tunnel that is exactly the
  film.** It was not in the first list. **ADDED.**

The shelf carries contaminants no query asks for but neighbouring words drag in: **cartoon 140 rows,
animation 67, chroma 52, greenscreen 9, render 23.** They are not in `forbidden_subjects` because they
are not *subjects*, and **every mechanical filter passed them.** Only the contact sheet caught them.

**Supply, measured:** 307 candidates → **403** after the corrections and `--per-query 10` → reviewable
set 173 → **242**. Thirty further probed queries were then added (119 total). Required: **265
distinct**. `--per-query 14` returns the same pool as 6, so **the lever is more probed queries, not a
higher cap.**

**Deliberately not used:** `sunset` (546 rows) and `sunrise` (202) are the two largest untapped blocks
on the shelf and both are banned by `era_setting`. This film has no golden hour in it.
