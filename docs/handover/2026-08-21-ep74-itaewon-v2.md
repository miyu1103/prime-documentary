## The one thing worth reading if you read nothing else

`AB-02` said no appellate judgment could be verified and required a re-check before ship. Six searches
had failed. The seventh asked a different question — **not "what did it decide" but "was it
postponed"** — and the answer came back immediately:

> **Both appeals were stopped.** Seoul High Court **Criminal Division 13 (Baek Kang-jin)** suspended
> the police defendants' appeal on **14 July 2025**. **Criminal Division 9-1 (Gong Do-il)** suspended
> the district office chief's on **28 August 2025**. Both to wait for the special investigation
> commission, which had asked for it in writing on 1 July 2025 — and which has since extended itself
> to December 2027.

The bench's own reason, verbatim: *if what the special committee is doing is re-investigating the
facts, then it is right to try the case afterwards… if the procedures become fragmented, the harm to
the victims is aggravated.*

**The film's ending was "we could not find out". It is now "neither could the court."** That is the
whole episode's controlling idea — a rule written for events with organisers cannot see a crowd that
has none — closing on itself.

**The lesson is transferable and it is the reason this is written down:** six queries asked the
instrument for a *result*. The seventh asked whether the *event* had happened. When a search for an
outcome keeps returning the announcement of a date, stop asking what it decided.
