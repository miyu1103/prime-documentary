## 1. Phase One was read, and it is why this film has an afternoon

The previous session marked `SRC-0009` — the FSRI *Lahaina Fire Comprehensive Timeline Report*
(Phase One, 17 April 2024) — **NOT YET READ**, and named it in `AB-05` as the largest open hole in
the record. It was the right call to name it. It was also the difference between a film and a lecture:
Phase Two gives you 84 analytical findings, and **none of them is a clock**. Phase One is nothing but
a clock.

It has now been read in full text, and `EP75_lahaina_FACTS_LEDGER.v002.md` §11 carries 55 new rows
from it.

**How, so nobody spends that hour again.** `ag.hawaii.gov` 403s. UL Research Institutes also deposits
the report on figshare, which does not:

```
curl -sSL "https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/28062371" -o fig.json    # -> download_url
curl -sSL -A "Mozilla/5.0 (... Chrome/126.0)" \
     "https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/51306806" -o phase1.pdf     # 132 MB, 199 pages
```

**The trap, and it is a good one.** Most of the body text uses an embedded font encoding that makes
pypdf return every character **shifted down by 29 code points**. `ACKNOWLEDGMENTS` extracts as
`$&.12:/('*0(176`. A space extracts as `\x03`. And **every digit extracts as a control character**, so
a naive read of a 199-page timeline report shows `"leading to  fatalities"` and `"reported at  p.m."` —
a document that appears to contain no numbers at all. It would be very easy to skim that, conclude the
extraction failed, and go back to secondary sources. The decoder is eight lines (`p1_decode.py`, in
this session's scratchpad; the shift is +29, applied per line, leaving already-plain lines alone).

**The five things in it that the film is now built on**, all `VERBATIM`:

| row | fact |
|---|---|
| LH-250 | "High winds crippled the communications infrastructure, **destroying cellular phone communication within the Lahaina region in the late morning of August 8, 2023**" |
| LH-255 | The **first** evacuation order in MEMA's own public-alert log naming a Lahaina subdivision is timestamped **16:16** — an hour and twenty-one minutes after the fire was reported, and hours after the phones died |
| LH-220/221 | The morning crews "reported that the fire was extinguished and returned to quarters at **14:17**." The afternoon fire was reported at **14:55**, at the same place. **Thirty-eight minutes** |
| LH-290/292 | "The only missing EOC sign-in sheet is the one for MEMA personnel for August 8, 2023. Maui County has not produced this document after multiple requests." … "Because of missing data, it is difficult to make a complete and accurate accounting of activities within the EOC" |
| LH-263 | ~16:45: a locked gate, 30–50 cars trapped behind it, a resident's reciprocating saw, and an officer who "rammed his body into the cyclone gate to open the other side." 30–50 fully loaded cars went through |

`LH-205` also retires half of `AB-06`: the film may now say **"forecast gusts of up to sixty miles an
hour,"** because that is primary and dated. It still may not say what the wind actually *did* — no
measured wind speed exists in this ledger.

## 2. ⛔-11 fired, and section 9 of the ledger was already wrong

The ledger required section 9 to be re-verified on the day of writing. It was, and **it had gone
stale in the eight days since it was written.**

v001 said the first of four annual settlement payments "was expected to begin flowing in July or
August 2026." Measured 2026-08-21 against two fetched sources:

- **No payment has been made.** Three attorneys and one fire survivor are challenging the ruling on
  attorneys' fees; filed 2 July 2026 with the Intermediate Court of Appeals, elevated to the **Hawaiʻi
  Supreme Court on 5 August 2026**. *"While the appeal is pending, no settlement payments can be
  made."* (Honolulu Star-Advertiser, 16 August 2026 — `SRC-0013`, fetched and read.)
- ~$1.1 billion is in trust for the first instalment. 94,816 claims by 21,750 claimants.
- v001's framing that the February 2026 ruling removed "the last legal obstacle" is **not correct** and
  was never correct — counsel said at the time that two other appeals were already pending.

**`LH-101…LH-103` of v001 may not be narrated.** v002 §10 replaces them with `LH-110…LH-117`, and the
script states the status **procedurally and with its date attached** ("As of August 2026, no payment
has been made"), so that it ages into a true sentence rather than a false one.

**This is not discharged.** ⛔-11 is satisfied for 2026-08-21 only. The court could rule in September.
**Re-verify §10 again before the packaging is written, and again before the video is scheduled.**

## 6. A real defect found, not fixed, and deliberately not papered over

`verify_script_lint.py` and `verify_script_structure.py` both returned **SKIP** on this episode.
Spec v3 §2.4 already calls `script_structure` decoration — 0 pass, 0 fail, 8 skips, **never once
executed**. Measured here, the cause is concrete and it is not a path bug:

- both look for `episodes/<EPID>/03_script/script.en.v*.md`;
- **no episode from PD-2026-062 onward has a `03_script` directory at all** — the authoring
  convention since then is `_planning/EPnn_<slug>_script.en.vNNN.md`;
- and the lint extracts spoken text from `[VO:]` lines, which this convention does not use.

So the two gates are looking for an artefact shape the channel stopped producing about thirteen
episodes ago. **They were not made to pass by copying the script into `03_script`** — that would
create a second copy of the script that drifts, and it would convert an honest SKIP into a green that
means nothing. `check_script_craft.py` reads the file where it actually lives, and it is the gate that
ran.

**The fix belongs to whoever next touches the acceptance code**: point both at
`_planning/EP*_<slug>_script.en.v*.md`, and teach the lint to strip `<!-- … -->` and `【…】` instead of
looking for `[VO:]`. That is the same defect as spec v3 §6.6 (`check_script_length` counting citation
comments as spoken words), and both are one function.

## 8. The nine things a human has to confirm, because no gate measures them

They are FILM BIBLE §17 and they are not decoration. The three that will actually decide whether this
film is right:

1. **That no frame, line, caption, card, title or thumbnail ever completes the sentence "if the sirens
   had sounded…".** No source establishes it. The film's shape is: the system existed, it was for
   this among other things, one siren inside the perimeter was operable, it had never been used for a
   fire, and it was not used — **then stop**.
2. **That the film never uses the shape *raise a question, leave it open*.** That shape, not any false
   claim, is what this topic's conspiracy audience is made of. Every question the script raises is
   answered inside ninety seconds with a date and a source.
3. **That the 14:17 → 14:55 gap never sits next to a word implying fault**, and that the County's
   statement about its firefighters' diligence and Finding 67 on mopup are in the same passage every
   time it is spoken.
