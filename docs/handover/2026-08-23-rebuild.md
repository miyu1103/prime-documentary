   live arm (52 ids locked until 2026-09-07). `pd_brief.py` prints itself at SessionStart.
4. **Decisions expire.** `check_decisions.py`: Review-by + Revoke-if required from 2026-08-23
   (13 decisions: 2 ok, 11 legacy, 0 expired). ADR-0011 (AE from EP77) got its dates.
