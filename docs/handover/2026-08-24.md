## 3. The wrong-country clips are the same defect three episodes in a row

EP71 had a legible `STILLWATER` water tower. EP72's pool held a British GWR passenger train. EP74
shipped a Shenzhen skyline, Moscow traffic, a Taiwanese street with Chinese signage, a Chinatown
market and a man in deep snow — in a film about Seoul on 29 October 2022.

**The shelf is searched with words like `street`, `city`, `traffic`, and those words do not carry a
country.** No machine on the ship path reads a country off a frame. The only thing that has ever
caught it is a person reading contact sheets at full size, which is why the sheet-reading step is
not optional and not delegable to a green tick.

## 4. Instruments that lied today

* **`caption_format` measured a file no film ships.** It took the newest `.srt` in `08_edit`;
  EP75's newest is a 907-cue working split, and the film carries the 533-cue `captions.final`. Now
  resolved from the filmconfig, which names it.
* **`pd_preflight`'s "near-black cut assets" scans the POOL, not the cuts.** It blocked three
  episodes on clips that are in no cut while `pd_prerender_gate`, which does read the cuts, passed
  all three. Message corrected.
* **20 stale contact sheets from a previous pool sat in `pool_frames/lacmegantic/`.** I read them
  and reported "the back third of EP72's pool is off-register" — **that was wrong and is
  withdrawn**. The receipt says 50 sheets; the disk had 70. The count disagreeing with the receipt
  was the tell, and I missed it until the titles contradicted what I thought I had seen.
* **`check_thumb_subject_luma`'s outline metric could only see a painted stroke.** Ring 1 is always
  the glyph's antialiasing, so the count broke there; every thumbnail the channel ships scored 0,
  including the live EP77–82 set. Now skips the antialias band and credits plain contrast; white
  type on a white field still scores 0.
* **The 150 px text floor is not evidence.** The thumbnail lane measured the channel's live
  best-CTR thumbnail at ~94 px. My floor came from one EP31 pair. **Their evidence wins; the floor
  is not enforced against that style**, and where their thumbnails fail it, the failure is recorded
  as the check's.
